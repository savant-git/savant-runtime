
# savant enterprise intelligence authority

status: user-directed architectural correction and proposed implementation specification
date: 2026-10-13

## 1. governing correction

carbon and straub are completely separate systems.

neither system owns, contains, or derives its authority from the other.

earlier representations placing straub beneath carbon are superseded by the user's explicit directive.

this correction does not establish a new implementation relationship, integration contract, or physical deployment arrangement.

any interaction between carbon and straub requires an explicitly authorized interface and preserves their independent ownership boundaries.

## 2. straub identity

straub retains its established responsibility for creating and operating datrices.

straub is not a datrix.

a datrix composes canonical substance, instances, typed relationships, immutable history, and deterministic projections.

existing verified behavior, compatibility, lineage, provenance, recovery, and replay must survive all extensions.

physical storage selection and datrix allocation policy remain unresolved.

## 3. straub enterprise refinements

the following refinements are implementation requirements subject to compatibility with verified runtime behavior:

1. canonical composition contracts prevent duplicate authority.
2. projection contracts identify authoritative inputs and source revisions.
3. dependency-aware invalidation minimizes unnecessary recomputation.
4. transaction boundaries preserve atomicity where supported.
5. permission-preserving composition prevents unauthorized disclosure.
6. immutable evolution retains prior accepted states.
7. idempotent mutation contracts distinguish retries from new changes.
8. deterministic recovery reconstructs derived state.
9. bounded queries protect resource availability.
10. cross-datrix references preserve identity and provenance.

these refinements extend existing primitives rather than requiring independent subsystems.

## 4. company ai identity

the company ai system is a distinct organizational intelligence capability.

its purpose is to discover, evaluate, propose, coordinate, and measure improvements across a company without acquiring independent authority over the company's underlying records or operations.

its governing cycle is:

observe → substantiate → compose → discover → hypothesize → compare → challenge → authorize → execute → measure → retain

model-generated conclusions remain proposals or claims until supported by applicable evidence and authority.

## 7. organizational invariants

accepted organizational constraints must be represented through existing authoritative primitives.

proposals must preserve applicable financial, contractual, operational, security, strategic, and brand constraints.

a proposal that conflicts with an applicable invariant cannot be executed without an authorized superseding decision.

## 8. consequence horizons

proposals must distinguish immediate consequences from longer-term estimates.

uncertainty must remain explicit.

estimated future consequences must never be represented as observed outcomes.

## 9. opportunity competition

proposals competing for the same resources must be evaluated together when their dependencies or consequences materially interact.

the system may recommend postponement, rejection, or no action.

## 10. counterfactual accountability

decision records must preserve considered alternatives, supporting assumptions, authorization, and subsequent observations.

unselected alternatives remain hypothetical.

## 11. bounded autonomy

autonomous execution requires explicit authorization specifying:

- permitted actions;
- applicable organizational scope;
- duration or expiration;
- resource limits;
- risk boundaries;
- revocation conditions.

model confidence does not confer authority.

## 12. architectural boundaries

carbon remains independent.

straub retains its established datrix responsibilities.

datrices do not become independent organizational decision authorities.

the company ai system consumes authorized information and produces governed proposals and execution requests.

external models, frameworks, protocols, and databases remain implementation resources rather than architectural authorities.

## 13. complexity ceiling

do not introduce:

- duplicate organizational truth stores;
- competing authority registries;
- independent agent hierarchies for every department;
- a new physical database without demonstrated necessity;
- a proprietary programming language solely for company ai;
- permanent storage of rebuildable simulations;
- universal prediction machinery without defensible models;
- cross-company access that bypasses authorization.

additional machinery requires demonstrated capability, compatibility, or operational benefit.

## 14. implementation admission

this document defines the corrected architectural boundaries and intended capabilities.

it does not establish runtime verification.

each implementation must be admitted against current authoritative graph state, accepted decisions, verified implementation, and applicable constitutional canon.

unresolved conflicts remain explicit until resolved by appropriate authority.
