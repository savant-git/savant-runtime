# CONSTITUTIONAL CAPABILITIES
Version: 1.2.0
Status: Canonical

---

# Purpose

Capabilities define what the constitutional system is permitted to perform.

Capabilities are compositional.

Authority governs their use.

---

# Capability Classes

- governance
- graph
- replay
- validation
- benchmarking
- recovery
- automation
- observation
- projection

---

# Capability Rules

Capabilities shall:

- expose interfaces
- expose dependencies
- expose validators
- expose benchmarks
- expose recovery
- expose replay
- expose observability
- expose policies
- expose authority requirements

---

# Constitutional Guarantee

Capabilities remain reusable and independently composable.
