# SAVANT CONSTITUTION
## Canonical Constitutional Authority
Version: 1.2.0
Status: Canonical
Authority: Constitutional Canon

# Purpose

This constitution governs Savant's architecture, implementation, evolution, and operation.

Its objectives are to maximize:

- correctness
- determinism
- auditability
- recoverability
- composability
- replayability
- extensibility
- semantic stability
- authority integrity

The constitution is itself governed.

It may evolve.

It may never self-authorize.

---

# Constitutional Laws

## Law 1 — Authority

Authority always precedes inference.

Authority always precedes projection.

Authority always precedes convenience.

Authority always precedes implementation.

Authority always precedes naming.

Authority is never manufactured.

Authority is admitted.

Authority is preserved.

Authority is superseded only through governed acceptance.

---

## Law 2 — Single Authority

Every authoritative fact exists exactly once.

Duplicate authority is forbidden.

Duplicate storage is acceptable only when explicitly projected.

Every projection must remain disposable.

Every projection must be regenerable.

---

## Law 3 — Emergent Modular edifice

Atomic characters are the only directly substantiated primitives.

They exist exactly once.

Everything above the atomic layer is composed entirely from reusable instances.

Nothing above the atomic layer may duplicate lower-level substance.

Composition is preferred over duplication.

Projection is preferred over storage.

Emergence is preferred over hardcoding.

---

## Law 4 — Instances

Instances are the universal building blocks.

Instances inherit identity.

Instances inherit lineage.

Instances inherit provenance.

Instances inherit authority.

Instances may be attached.

Instances may be projected.

Instances remain independently reusable.

- succession

## Law 6 — Living Constitution

The constitution continuously evaluates itself.

- redundancy
- contradiction
- obsolete rules
- missing authority
- missing lineage
- missing provenance
- missing dependencies
- architectural drift
- dead ends

It may propose improvements.

It may never automatically adopt them.

Acceptance remains governed.

## Law 8 — Deterministic Evolution

New admitted information automatically triggers:

- authority validation
- schema validation
- semantic classification
- dependency analysis
- downstream propagation
- upstream invalidation
- task recomputation
- completion reevaluation
- priority recomputation
- replay verification

Every element must satisfy constitutional quality checkpoints before higher composition.

1. structural validity

## Law 10 — Upgrades

Every modification is compared against the previous accepted implementation.

Reject any change that:

- reduces functionality
- weakens authority
- duplicates primitives
- breaks replay
- destroys lineage
- destroys provenance
- creates dead ends
- reduces extensibility
- introduces unexplained regressions

Every accepted upgrade leaves Savant measurably stronger.

---

## Law 11 — Evidence

Implementation is not completion.

Completion requires evidence.

Evidence may include:

- tests
- validators
- receipts
- replay
- integrity
- schema validation
- policy validation
- accepted authority

Assertions are never evidence.

## Law 12 — Continuous Improvement

Savant continuously evaluates itself.

It searches for:

- simplification
- normalization
- stronger composition
- reduced duplication
- stronger authority
- better replay
- greater determinism
- architectural improvements
- constitutional improvements

Every accepted improvement strengthens future evolution without sacrificing recoverability.
