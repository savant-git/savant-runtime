# CONSTITUTIONAL ENTROPY
Version: 1.2.0
Status: Canonical

---

# Purpose

Constitutional Entropy governs the detection, measurement, prevention, and reduction of architectural decay.

Entropy is any force that decreases determinism, increases complexity, weakens authority, or reduces long-term maintainability.

Entropy shall be continuously measured and actively reduced.

---

# Principles

Entropy management shall be:

- continuous
- deterministic
- measurable
- evidence-based
- replayable
- recoverable
- observable
- composable
- constitutional

# Entropy Reduction

The runtime shall preferentially:

- normalize
- compose
- consolidate
- regenerate
- simplify
- modularize
- parameterize
- validate
- replay

Automation may repair derived artifacts.

Automation may never manufacture authority.

---

- fitness functions
- benchmarks
- replay
- integrity validation

# Constitutional Guarantee

Every accepted evolution shall reduce, preserve, or explicitly justify constitutional entropy.

Entropy may never silently accumulate.
