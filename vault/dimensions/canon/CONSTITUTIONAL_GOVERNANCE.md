# Purpose

This document defines how constitutional authority is created, amended, interpreted, validated, superseded, and enforced.

No constitutional rule becomes authoritative without governed acceptance.

---

Every constitutional rule shall be:

- authoritative
- deterministic
- replayable
- composable
- observable
- testable
- recoverable
- versioned
- auditable

# Constitutional Amendment Lifecycle

Every amendment follows the same lifecycle.

## Phase 1

Proposal

Record:

- motivation
- affected authorities
- expected benefits
- expected risks

---

## Phase 2

Analysis

Automatically determine:

- constitutional conflicts
- dependency impact
- compatibility
- replay impact
- validator impact
- implementation impact

---

## Phase 3

Simulation

Generate projected constitutional state.

No authority changes occur.

---

## Phase 4

Validation

Run:

- schema validation
- policy validation
- integrity validation
- replay validation
- benchmark comparison

---

## Phase 5

Acceptance

Authority explicitly accepts or rejects.

AI never self-accepts constitutional changes.

---

## Phase 6

Mutation

Accepted amendment becomes authoritative.

Previous authority remains preserved as immutable history.

---

## Phase 7

Projection

Regenerate all derived artifacts.

---

## Phase 8

Verification

Replay entire authority graph.

All validators must pass.

---

## Phase 9

Attestation

Generate:

- receipts
- evidence
- findings
- integrity reports
- replay reports

- manufacture authority

- bypass replay
- bypass validation

# Constitutional Interpretation

Interpretation always favors:

- explicit authority
- deterministic behavior
- minimal authority
- reversible evolution
- preservation of history

Inference never overrides authority.

# Constitutional Conflict Resolution

When conflicts exist:

1. Detect conflict.
2. Preserve competing sources.
3. Compare authority level.
4. Reject silent reconciliation.
5. Require explicit acceptance.
6. Preserve historical lineage.
7. Regenerate projections.
8. Replay graph.
9. Record findings.
