# CONSTITUTIONAL REASONING
Version: 1.2.0
Status: Canonical

---

# Purpose

Reasoning derives conclusions from constitutional knowledge.

Reasoning never manufactures authority.

---

# Reasoning Pipeline

1. Collect
2. Validate
3. Classify
4. Analyze
5. Infer
6. Verify
7. Benchmark
8. Explain
9. Publish

---

# Reasoning Constraints

Reasoning shall:

- preserve authority
- preserve determinism
- preserve replay
- expose assumptions
- expose uncertainty
- expose evidence
- expose confidence
- expose lineage
- expose provenance

---

# Constitutional Guarantee

Reasoning remains transparent, reproducible, and non-authoritative.
