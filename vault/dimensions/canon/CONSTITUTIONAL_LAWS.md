Version: 1.2.0

# Purpose

The Constitutional Laws define the immutable operating principles governing every subsystem within Savant.

They are constitutional constraints rather than implementation details.

Every implementation, validator, policy, runtime, projection, graph mutation, and architectural evolution shall conform to these laws.
