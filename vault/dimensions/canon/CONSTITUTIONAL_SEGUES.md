# CONSTITUTIONAL SEGUES
Version: 1.2.0
Status: Canonical

---

# Purpose

Typed Segues are the sole constitutional relationship primitive within Savant.

Objects possess authority.

Segues define relationships.

Nothing is related implicitly.

Every relationship shall be represented by an explicit typed segue.

---

# Principles

Segues shall always be:

- explicit
- directional
- typed
- deterministic
- replayable
- composable
- observable
- versioned
- constitutional

---

# Universal Structure

Every segue exposes:

- identifier
- segue type
- source object
- destination object
- authority source
- creation event
- lineage
- provenance
- semantic digest

---

# Constitutional Laws

Every segue shall:

- connect exactly two identities
- possess exactly one canonical type
- preserve authority
- preserve determinism
- preserve replayability
- preserve lineage
- preserve provenance
- support validation
- remain graph-addressable

# Validation

Every segue validates:

- source identity
- destination identity
- segue type
- authority
- lineage
- provenance
- constitutional legality
- graph integrity
- replay compatibility

---

# Projection Rules

Reverse relationships shall be projected whenever possible.

Authoritative reverse edges shall not be duplicated unless constitutionally required.

---

# Forbidden Operations

Segues shall never:

- manufacture authority
- bypass governance
- bypass validation
- hide contradictions
- duplicate authority
- duplicate lineage
- duplicate provenance
- introduce cycles violating policy
- become anonymous

---

# Evolution

New segue types require:

- constitutional proposal
- impact analysis
- validator updates
- replay verification
- benchmark verification
- governed acceptance
- schema update
- graph update
- projection regeneration

# Constitutional Guarantee

Every relationship inside Savant is explicit.

Every relationship is typed.

Every relationship is replayable.

Nothing is connected implicitly.
