Version: 1.2.0

# Purpose

Constraints define constitutional invariants that no implementation may violate.

---

# Universal Constraints

No implementation may:

- duplicate authority
- bypass governance
- bypass replay
- bypass validation
- bypass benchmarks
- erase history
- destroy lineage
- destroy provenance
- manufacture authority

---

# Constitutional Guarantee

Constraints are permanent unless constitutionally amended.
