# CONSTITUTIONAL PROVENANCE
Version: 1.2.0
Status: Canonical

---

# Purpose

Provenance records where constitutional information originated.

Authority without provenance is invalid.

---

# Provenance Records

Record:

- originating authority
- source
- admission event
- governing decision
- evidence
- validators
- timestamps
- supersession
- constitutional status

---

# Guarantee

Every accepted fact is traceable to its governing source.
