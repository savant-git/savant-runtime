# CONSTITUTIONAL ADMISSIONS
Version: 1.2.0
Status: Canonical

---

# Purpose

Admissions govern how new authority enters Savant.

Nothing becomes authority automatically.

---

# Admission Requirements

Admission requires:

- source
- authority
- evidence
- schema validation
- semantic validation
- replay compatibility
- benchmark compatibility
- governance
- graph registration

---

# Admission Pipeline

1. Receive
2. Classify
3. Validate
4. Analyze
5. Benchmark
6. Replay
7. Govern
8. Admit
9. Record

---

# Guarantee

Authority is admitted.

Never discovered.
