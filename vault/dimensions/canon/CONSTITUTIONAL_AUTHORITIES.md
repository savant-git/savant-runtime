# CONSTITUTIONAL AUTHORITIES
Version: 1.2.0
Status: Canonical

---

# Purpose

This document defines what constitutes authority within Savant.

Authority determines what is accepted as truth.

Storage location, implementation, popularity, or recency never establish authority.

---

# Constitutional Principles

Authority shall always be:

- explicit
- minimal
- deterministic
- replayable
- observable
- recoverable
- composable
- auditable
- versioned

Authority is admitted.

It is never inferred.

---

# Authority edifice

Higher authority always supersedes lower authority.

Authority precedence:

1. Explicit project-owner directive
2. Accepted authoritative graph
3. Accepted decisions
4. Constitutional canon
5. Verified implementation
6. Admitted evidence
7. Deterministic projections
8. Historical implementation
9. Historical documents
10. Engineering inference
11. Speculation

---

# Authority Admission

Authority enters the system only through governed acceptance.

Admission requires:

- explicit source
- constitutional validity
- schema validity
- identity assignment
- provenance
- lineage
- validation
- replay compatibility
- graph registration

---

# Authority Identity

Every authority exposes:

- identifier
- authority class
- authority source
- semantic digest
- creation event
- lineage
- provenance
- supersession history
- validation history

Identity is immutable.

Authority evolves through governed mutation.

---

# Authority Mutation

Every mutation shall:

1. resolve authority
2. validate authority
3. validate constitution
4. validate schema
5. validate policy
6. update graph
7. regenerate projections
8. verify replay
9. record evidence

---

# Authority Supersession

Authority may be superseded.

It is never overwritten.

Supersession records preserve:

- previous authority
- replacement authority
- governing decision
- timestamp
- lineage
- rationale
- replay compatibility
- historical accessibility
- recovery capability

---

# Authority Constraints

Authority shall never:

- originate from inference
- originate from implementation
- originate from projections
- originate from documentation
- originate from popularity
- originate from convenience
- originate from automation
- originate from AI output
- originate from replay

---

# AI Authority

AI may produce:

- observations
- proposals
- analysis
- extraction
- implementation
- validation
- estimates
- projections
- recommendations

AI output possesses no authority until explicitly admitted.

---

# Conflict Resolution

When competing authorities exist:

1. Detect conflict.
2. Preserve both.
3. Compare authority level.
4. Reject silent reconciliation.
5. Require governed acceptance.
6. Record supersession.
7. Replay graph.
8. Regenerate projections.
9. Record findings.

---

# Authority Verification

Authority continuously verifies:

- constitutional validity
- schema validity
- provenance
- lineage
- dependency integrity
- replay integrity
- semantic consistency
- graph consistency
- policy compliance

---

# Constitutional Guarantee

Authority remains explicit, minimal, deterministic, and recoverable.

Every accepted truth can be traced to its governing source.

Nothing becomes constitutional merely because it exists.
