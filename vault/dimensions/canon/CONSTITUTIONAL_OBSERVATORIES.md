# CONSTITUTIONAL OBSERVATORIES
Version: 1.2.0
Status: Canonical

---

# Purpose

Constitutional Observatories continuously observe, measure, analyze, explain, and report the health of the entire Savant ecosystem.

Observatories never create authority.

They expose reality.

---

# Principles

Observatories shall be:

- continuous
- deterministic
- replayable
- observable
- evidence-driven
- composable
- recoverable
- historical
- constitutional
