# CONSTITUTIONAL SUCCESSION
Version: 1.2.0
Status: Canonical

---

# Purpose

Succession governs replacement while preserving constitutional history.

Nothing is replaced destructively.

---

# Succession Rules

Successors shall preserve:

- identity
- lineage
- provenance
- authority chain
- replay
- recovery
- benchmarks
- observability
- history

Superseded objects remain historical.

---

# Constitutional Guarantee

Evolution occurs through succession rather than replacement.
