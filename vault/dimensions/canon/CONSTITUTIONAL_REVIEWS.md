# CONSTITUTIONAL REVIEWS
Version: 1.2.0
Status: Canonical

---

# Purpose

Constitutional Reviews provide the mandatory evaluation performed before every accepted mutation.

Reviews determine whether a proposal strengthens Savant while preserving constitutional integrity.

Every accepted change shall answer every review question.

---

# Principles

Reviews shall always be:

- objective
- deterministic
- evidence-based
- replayable
- observable
- benchmarked
- constitutional
- repeatable
- auditable

---

# Eighteen Constitutional Review Questions

## Authority

### 1. Authority

Does the proposal preserve constitutional authority boundaries?

---

### 2. Evidence

Is every asserted change supported by admissible evidence?

---

## Architecture

### 3. Composition

Does the proposal improve composition instead of duplication?

---

### 4. Normalization

Does it reduce authoritative duplication?

---

## Determinism

### 5. Replay

Can the system still replay deterministically?

---

### 6. Recovery

Can every derived artifact still be regenerated?

---

## Graph

### 7. Dependencies

Have all dependency impacts been analyzed?

---

### 8. Lineage

Are lineage and provenance completely preserved?

---

## Quality

### 9. Benchmarks

Does the proposal outperform or equal the accepted baseline?

---

### 10. Fitness

Does every architectural fitness function remain healthy?

---

## Runtime

### 11. Automation

Does automation become simpler, safer, or more reliable?

---

### 12. Observability

Does observability improve without introducing new authority?

---

## Evolution

### 13. Extensibility

Does the proposal improve future extension?

---

### 14. Composability

Does it improve recursive composition?

---

## Economics

### 15. Lifetime Cost

Does total lifetime ownership cost decrease?

---

### 16. Entropy

Does constitutional entropy decrease or remain justified?

---

## Constitution

### 17. Governance

Does governance remain explicit and constitutional?

---

### 18. Improvement

Will Savant be objectively better after acceptance?

---

# Required Evidence

Each review shall include:

- authority digest
- benchmark comparison
- replay result
- validator results
- dependency analysis
- impact analysis
- constitutional analysis
- evidence references
- acceptance recommendation

---

# Automatic Failure

The review fails immediately if it detects:

- authority violation
- replay failure
- validator failure
- benchmark regression
- integrity failure
- dependency corruption
- constitutional violation
- unexplained regression
- missing evidence

---

# Acceptance

Acceptance requires:

- all review questions answered
- evidence complete
- benchmarks passed
- replay passed
- validators passed
- governance satisfied
- authority preserved
- constitutional compliance verified
- accepted decision recorded

---

# Constitutional Guarantee

Every accepted mutation is reviewed from every constitutional perspective before becoming part of Savant.

No implementation bypasses constitutional review.
