# CONSTITUTIONAL REFACTORING
Version: 1.2.0
Status: Canonical

---

# Purpose

Constitutional Refactoring governs every structural improvement to Savant.

Its objective is continuous architectural improvement without altering accepted authority, meaning, behavior, or replay.

Refactoring changes implementation.

It does not change constitutional truth.

---

# Principles

Refactoring shall always be:

- deterministic
- reversible
- replayable
- evidence-based
- composable
- observable
- recoverable
- authority-preserving
- benchmarked

---

# Nine Constitutional Refactoring Laws

1. Authority Preservation
2. Behavioral Preservation
3. Semantic Preservation
4. Normalization
5. Modular Composition
6. Dependency Integrity
7. Replay Preservation
8. Evolution Readiness
9. Continuous Improvement

---

# Authority Preservation

Refactoring shall never:

- alter authority
- manufacture authority
- delete authority
- rewrite accepted decisions
- destroy provenance
- destroy lineage
- weaken governance
- bypass admission
- bypass constitutional policy

---

# Behavioral Preservation

Accepted observable behavior shall remain equivalent unless an accepted decision explicitly authorizes change.

Verify:

- interfaces
- outputs
- validators
- replay
- integrations
- compatibility
- projections
- recovery
- policies

---

# Semantic Preservation

Preserve:

- meaning
- terminology
- identity
- relationships
- graph structure
- constitutional intent
- dependency semantics
- projection semantics
- historical interpretation

---

# Normalization

Refactoring shall reduce:

- duplication
- redundant storage
- duplicated authority
- repeated logic
- repeated validation
- repeated policies
- repeated schemas
- repeated relationships
- repeated maintenance

Normalization shall never reduce correctness.

---

# Modular Composition

Prefer:

- instances
- typed segues
- parameterization
- composition
- reusable validators
- reusable policies
- reusable schemas
- reusable projections
- reusable behaviors

---

# Dependency Integrity

Every refactoring validates:

- dependency correctness
- propagation
- ordering
- compatibility
- reachability
- cycle detection
- graph integrity
- replay integrity
- recovery integrity

---

# Replay Preservation

Every refactoring must preserve:

- deterministic replay
- graph reconstruction
- manifest regeneration
- projection regeneration
- integrity trees
- digests
- validators
- evidence
- recovery

Replay failure blocks acceptance.

---

# Evolution Readiness

Every accepted refactoring shall improve:

- extensibility
- modularity
- maintainability
- observability
- composability
- recoverability
- constitutional clarity
- future optionality
- migration safety

---

# Continuous Improvement

Every refactoring compares against the previous accepted implementation.

Reject any change that:

- increases duplication
- weakens authority
- reduces replayability
- increases maintenance
- creates dead ends
- breaks composition
- reduces determinism
- destroys lineage
- introduces unexplained regressions

---

# Automatic Refactoring Candidates

The runtime may identify opportunities involving:

- duplication
- obsolete abstractions
- dead implementations
- fragmented composition
- dependency simplification
- projection simplification
- normalization
- validator consolidation
- policy consolidation

Identification is automatic.

Acceptance remains governed.

---

# Constitutional Guarantee

Every accepted refactoring leaves Savant measurably more deterministic, composable, maintainable, replayable, recoverable, and constitutionally consistent than before.
