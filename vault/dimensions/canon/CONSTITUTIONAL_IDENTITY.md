# CONSTITUTIONAL IDENTITY
Version: 1.2.0
Status: Canonical

---

# Purpose

Identity is the permanent semantic anchor of every constitutional object.

Identity survives implementation, migration, replay, recovery, and supersession.

---

# Identity Principles

Identity shall be:

- immutable
- unique
- semantic
- replayable
- recoverable
- observable
- version-independent
- authority-aware
- constitutional

---

# Identity Fields

Every identity exposes:

- identifier
- semantic identity
- authority source
- lineage
- provenance
- creation event
- supersession chain
- validation history
- semantic digest

---

# Identity Laws

Identity shall never:

- change meaning
- duplicate another identity
- lose lineage
- lose provenance
- depend upon implementation
- depend upon storage
- depend upon filenames
- depend upon projections
- depend upon runtime

---

# Guarantee

Identity remains constant throughout the lifetime of constitutional authority.
