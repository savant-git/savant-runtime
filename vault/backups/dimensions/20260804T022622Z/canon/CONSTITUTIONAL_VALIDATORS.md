# CONSTITUTIONAL VALIDATORS
Version: 1.2.0
Status: Canonical

---

# Purpose

Validators are the constitutional proof mechanism.

Nothing is accepted merely because it exists.

Everything accepted is validated.

---

# Principles

Validators shall be:

- deterministic
- replayable
- composable
- observable
- benchmarked
- evidence-based
- reusable
- versioned
- constitutional

---

# Constitutional Domains

Validators verify:

- authority
- schema
- semantics
- dependencies
- replay
- integrity
- benchmarks
- policies
- recovery

---

# Requirements

Every validator exposes:

- identifier
- scope
- inputs
- outputs
- evidence
- failure conditions
- benchmark history
- lineage
- provenance

---

# Failure

Validator failure blocks constitutional acceptance.

---

# Guarantee

Validation precedes governance.

Governance precedes acceptance.
