# Projection Canon

Commands, capabilities, modules, engines, context, registry, UI, reports, exports, and files are projections unless explicitly marked as authority.

Projections must be reproducible and disposable.

Source dumps and their manifests are evidence projections. A matching content hash proves snapshot fidelity only; it does not make the captured artifact authoritative. Redacted, skipped, truncated, or excluded material remains explicitly unknown and must never be reconstructed as canon from inference.
