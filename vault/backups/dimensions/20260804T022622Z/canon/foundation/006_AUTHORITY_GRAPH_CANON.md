# Authority Graph Canon

The authority graph contains:

- meta archetypes
- archetypes
- templates
- instances
- segues
- policies

The current authority surfaces are:

- `canon/foundation/` for foundational principles
- `authority_graph/` for structural definitions and policies
- `canon-system/authority/` for dynamic constitutional records

Everything else is projected from authority.

Presence in a source snapshot, context document, registry, report, export, or generated graph does not promote an artifact to authority. Authority is determined by its declared surface and provenance.
