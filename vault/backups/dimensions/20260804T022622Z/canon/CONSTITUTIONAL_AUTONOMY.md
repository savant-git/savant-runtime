# CONSTITUTIONAL AUTONOMY
Version: 1.2.0
Status: Canonical

---

# Purpose

Defines the constitutional limits of automation.

Automation may execute governed work.

Automation may never govern itself.

---

# Automation May

- validate
- benchmark
- replay
- regenerate
- project
- recover
- audit
- observe
- recommend

---

# Automation Shall Never

- manufacture authority
- admit authority
- amend the constitution
- rewrite accepted decisions
- override governance
- erase history
- fabricate evidence
- conceal failures
- approve itself

---

# Constitutional Guarantee

Automation remains an executor.

Governance remains the constitutional authority.
