# CONSTITUTIONAL QUALITY
Version: 1.2.0
Status: Canonical

---

# Purpose

Constitutional Quality establishes the minimum standards every Savant artifact must satisfy before it may participate in higher composition.

No object advances solely because it exists.

Admission requires demonstrated quality.

---

# Quality Principles

Quality shall always be:

- measurable
- deterministic
- repeatable
- observable
- replayable
- recoverable
- composable
- evidence-based
- continuously verified

---

# Nine Constitutional Checkpoints

Every artifact shall satisfy:

1. Structural validity
2. Semantic correctness
3. Authority validity
4. Dependency completeness
5. Deterministic behavior
6. Compatibility
7. Stability
8. Replay and recovery
9. Integration readiness
