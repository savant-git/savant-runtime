Version: 1.2.0

# Purpose

Normalization minimizes authoritative storage while maximizing deterministic regeneration.

---

# Normalization Laws

Every authoritative fact:

- exists exactly once
- has one identity
- has one authority
- has explicit lineage
- has explicit provenance
- is graph-addressable
- is replayable
- is recoverable
- is composable

---

# Constitutional Guarantee

Normalization continuously reduces duplication without reducing capability.
