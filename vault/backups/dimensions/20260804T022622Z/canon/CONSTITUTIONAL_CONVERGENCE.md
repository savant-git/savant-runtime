# CONSTITUTIONAL CONVERGENCE
Version: 1.2.0
Status: Canonical

---

# Purpose

Convergence guarantees that constitutional propagation reaches one deterministic steady state.

---

# Convergence Requirements

Propagation shall:

- terminate
- remain deterministic
- detect cycles
- detect oscillation
- detect contradiction
- preserve authority
- preserve replay
- preserve integrity
- preserve recovery

---

# Failure Conditions

Propagation fails upon:

- infinite recursion
- oscillation
- contradictory state
- unresolved dependency
- authority conflict
- replay divergence
- validator failure
- integrity failure
- benchmark failure

---

# Constitutional Guarantee

Every constitutional propagation converges to a deterministic fixed point or fails explicitly.
