# CONSTITUTIONAL POLICIES
Version: 1.2.0
Status: Canonical

---

# Purpose

Policies define constitutional constraints governing runtime behavior.

Policies constrain execution.

They never manufacture authority.

---

# Principles

Policies shall be:

- explicit
- deterministic
- replayable
- composable
- versioned
- observable
- benchmarked
- recoverable
- constitutional

---

# Policy Domains

Policies govern:

- authority
- validation
- replay
- projections
- automation
- governance
- recovery
- evolution
- observatories

---

# Requirements

Every policy exposes:

- identifier
- scope
- authority
- trigger
- evaluation
- outcome
- lineage
- provenance
- semantic digest

---

# Guarantee

Policies govern behavior.

Authority governs truth.
