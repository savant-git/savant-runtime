ABOUT THE DEMONSTRATION

See the process before running the system.

This standalone interface demonstrates session interaction, iteration progression, candidate comparison, and lineage. Its scores and events are generated locally from a fixed illustrative sequence. No AI providers, filesystem changes, or production Urge sessions are invoked.
