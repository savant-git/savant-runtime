# savant edifice level criteria: strengthened nine-field qualification

status: proposed, non-authoritative criteria revision
revision: 2026-10-18
baseline: savant edifice level criteria (2026-10-15); savant_structure_current_v4.1.1.md
source authority: savant_master_doctrine_edifice_classification_updated_20261016(1).md

## purpose and preservation

this is an **extension of all 27 existing nine-field records**, not a replacement with a generic contract. the existing three edifices, nine ranks, level names, atomic-glyph supersession, composition rule, and authority distinctions are preserved. the october 16 doctrine explicitly marks all level-exclusive functional predicates **unresolved**. the more discriminating functional predicates below are therefore **proposals** pending reconciliation with actual accepted module instances; they are not promoted to canon by publication.

**absolute modu preservation:** never change intrinsic functionality, core mechanics, accepted semantic identity, or valid behavior to make a modu fit a level definition. if an accepted placement disagrees with a proposed predicate, first correct the *proposed predicate* without weakening accepted constraints. change only nonexclusive metadata or relationships when compatible with authority, identity, and dependents. propose a level move only where its existing placement lacks controlling authority and actual accepted composition independently justifies the move. preserve unresolved authority conflicts rather than forcing alignment.

| 2 | mote | iota | line |

identity `glyph` supersedes historical identity `iota` at rank one; utility `iota` remains rank two. the source doctrine uses zero-based indices `00` to `08`; this document uses human-readable ranks `1` to `9` for the same positions, not additional levels.

## nine independent tests

| field | binding question | failure condition |
|---|---|---|
| functional | what exact observable semantic responsibility is claimed? | label or conventional word meaning substituted for behavior |
| qualifies | what positive, independently checkable facts admit this candidate? | necessary fact missing or inferred from a path |
| disqualifies | which counterexample or condition excludes it? | exclusion triggered by accepted evidence |
| composition | which canonical, distinct, participating predecessor instances produce it? | missing or wrong-rank participants; a call counted as composition |
| emergence | what new behavior depends on interaction, and how can it be demonstrated? | single constituent already supplies the complete result |
| boundaries | what semantic substance does this modu own, and what does it only use? | implementation/dependency mistaken for semantic ownership |
| exclusivity | why is this exact edifice and rank the sole supported classification? | accepted competing owner/rank or ambiguous evidence |
| evidence | which admitted records prove each required fact? | absent, stale, contradictory, or merely historical evidence |
| invariants | what must survive instantiation, replay, relocation, and projection? | identity, behavior, lineage, provenance, or compatibility loss |

**admission is conjunctive, not a score.** atomic glyphs must have a substantiated irreducibility witness. each higher level requires at least two *distinct participating instances* of its immediately preceding level in the same edifice, linked by accepted typed composition, with acyclic recursive ancestry. dependency, invocation, import, attachment, containment, and file placement are not substitutes for composition. the lower-level instances must causally participate in the claimed emergent behavior. do not infer that a higher rank implies larger files, greater performance, or conventional organizational seniority.

**exclusivity is structural plus semantic.** exact canonical composition supplies rank; accepted semantic ownership supplies edifice; level-specific behavior supplies a proposed functional distinction. shared behaviors such as reuse, coordination, and transformation can legitimately span ranks. until accepted module evidence distinguishes them, the proposed descriptions cannot guarantee semantic non-overlap.

## evidence-driven one-by-one reconciliation

for every established modu, read its accepted decision and current canonical instance/definition before proposing any change. record its canonical id, existing edifice/rank, governing authority, exact constituent ids and typed segues, observed functional contract, excluded alternative classifications, dependents, and proposed disposition.

1. **retain:** accepted placement and verified composition/behavior satisfy the accepted rules.
2. **refine proposed criteria:** established functionality and higher-authority placement are valid, but the proposed behavioral predicate is too narrow, too broad, or misdescribes the modu. preserve the modu and change the predicate.
3. **adjust nonexclusive representation:** only derived metadata, presentation, or nonintrinsic relationship encoding may change, without semantic/behavioral loss.
4. **propose reclassification:** verified accepted composition supports another existing level, the current placement is not higher-authority, and compatibility/lineage can be preserved. do not execute the move without the necessary authority.
5. **conflict or unresolved:** accepted sources disagree or evidence is missing. preserve original records and refuse an invented resolution.

**current evidence limit:** the october 18 snapshot and october 16 doctrine are insufficient here to certify every established modu's functional and composition contracts. path-level examples such as `edifices/identity/exiles/carbon/instance.yaml` and `edifices/utility/epiphany/universal_classifier.py` cannot prove the rank suggested by their locations. this document therefore does not claim any actual modu was relocated, reconciled, or modified.

## deterministic classification projection
