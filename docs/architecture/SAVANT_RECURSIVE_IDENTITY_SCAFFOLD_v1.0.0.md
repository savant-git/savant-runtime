# Savant Recursive Identity Scaffold

**Version:** 1.0.0 authority candidate  
**Date:** 2026-08-06  
**Mutation authorized:** No

## Authority and purpose

This document formalizes the universal substantiation criteria for Savant's nine identity tiers. It extends, but does not silently rewrite, the accepted recursive runtime decision. The accepted order is:

`iota → mote → trait → quirk → prodigal → exile → innate → portal → obelisk`

Every stable parent is composed from multiple distinct active instances of the tier immediately beneath it. Canonical child substance remains stored once; parents hold identity references, composition edges, contracts, and receipts rather than copies.

A tier is not established by path, filename, code size, directory depth, importance, age, or human preference. It is established by its constitutive criteria and its tier-specific emergent property.

## The mathematical composition law

Let `L₁ … L₉` be the ordered identity tiers and let `C(x)` be the direct constituent set of instance `x`. A substantiated instance at rank `r > 1` must satisfy:

1. every member of `C(x)` is a valid active instance of `Lᵣ₋₁`;
2. `|C(x)| ≥ 2`;
3. every required constituent is referenced by stable identity;
4. no constituent's canonical substance is copied into `x`;
5. the composition yields the rank-specific emergent property `Eᵣ`;
6. `Eᵣ` is not supplied by any one constituent alone;
7. the composition is deterministic or explicitly declares governed nondeterminism;
8. authority, lineage, provenance, dependency, compatibility, and validation evidence remain traversable from parent to every constituent;
9. failure to prove any condition leaves the element an incomplete candidate, not a substantiated instance.

The emergent sequence is:

| Rank | Tier | Emergence |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | iota | Identity |
| 2 | mote | Coherence |
| 3 | trait | Disposition |
| 4 | quirk | Behavior |
| 5 | prodigal | Capability |
| 6 | exile | Agency |
| 7 | innate | Faculty |
| 8 | portal | Access |
| 9 | obelisk | Sovereignty |

This sequence supplies symmetry without making tiers interchangeable: identity becomes coherence; coherence becomes disposition; disposition becomes behavior; behavior becomes capability; capability becomes agency; agency becomes faculty; faculty becomes governed access; governed access becomes sovereign system purpose.

## Universal instance criteria

1. Stable identity independent of path.
2. One archetype binding.
3. Direct child references with no duplicated substance.
4. Declared authority state.
5. Lineage and provenance.
6. Dependencies and dependents.
7. Compatibility and succession.
8. Extension and attachment support.
9. Deterministic projection and validation receipts.

## 1. Iota

**Constitutive essence:** The smallest identity-bearing canonical unit whose removal or division changes meaning.

**Emergent property:** Identity

**Canonical scope:** One indivisible semantic assertion, datum, tokenized rule, reference, parameter, or operation.

**Direct composition:** none; this is the atomic identity tier.

### Required criteria

1. It must possess a stable identity independent of path.
2. It must carry one authoritative semantic responsibility.
3. It must be indivisible without semantic loss or authority ambiguity.
4. It must declare type, value or operation, authority state, lineage, provenance, and compatibility.
5. It must be independently referenceable and reusable.
6. It must contain no lower identity-tier instances.
7. It must expose deterministic serialization or canonical representation.
8. It must fail closed when its meaning, authority, or type is unresolved.
9. It must remain immutable in identity; changes supersede rather than silently mutate meaning.

### Disqualifiers

- It must not bundle multiple independently meaningful responsibilities.
- It must not own an orchestration workflow.
- It must not pretend a file, field, byte, character, or AST node is automatically an iota.
- It must not derive authority from physical placement.

### Substantiation proof

A reviewer can state its complete meaning in one unambiguous sentence and cannot split it into two independently reusable identity-bearing units without changing that meaning.

A valid iota may become a constituent of a mote; it does not become one merely by being placed beneath a `motes/` path.

## 2. Mote

**Constitutive essence:** The smallest coherent micro-capability, formed from multiple iotas whose interaction produces one usable effect.

**Emergent property:** Coherence

**Canonical scope:** One bounded micro-function, predicate, transform, binding, invariant, or typed relation.

**Direct composition:** two or more distinct active iota instances.

### Required criteria

1. It must compose at least two distinct active iota instances.
2. It must produce one coherent effect not supplied by any constituent iota alone.
3. It must define input, output, preconditions, postconditions, and failure semantics where executable.
4. It must preserve direct references to constituent iotas rather than copying their substance.
5. It must expose deterministic composition order or explicitly commutative composition.
6. It must remain independently testable and reusable.
7. It must declare internal dependency direction and reject cycles unless a higher authority explicitly permits a fixed point.
8. It must emit or support evidence sufficient to prove its micro-capability.
9. It must remain narrower than an observable enduring property.

### Disqualifiers

- It must not be a renamed iota.
- It must not contain unrelated effects.
- It must not claim a stable behavioral characteristic that belongs at trait level.
- It must not duplicate constituent iotas.

### Substantiation proof

Removing any required iota breaks the micro-capability, while the remaining composition still cannot be reclassified as the same mote.

A valid mote may become a constituent of a trait; it does not become one merely by being placed beneath a `traits/` path.

## 3. Trait

**Constitutive essence:** A stable, observable property produced by multiple motes and consistently expressed across applicable contexts.

**Emergent property:** Disposition

**Canonical scope:** One durable quality, guarantee, tendency, constraint, interface property, or measurable characteristic.

**Direct composition:** two or more distinct active mote instances.

### Required criteria

1. It must compose at least two distinct active mote instances.
2. It must express one stable property observable through a declared surface.
3. It must define the contexts in which the property is guaranteed and those in which it is inapplicable.
4. It must include measurable acceptance conditions.
5. It must remain stable across repeated executions with equivalent inputs and authority state.
6. It must declare conflicts and precedence among constituent motes.
7. It must preserve constituent identity and provenance.
8. It must support property-level validation rather than example-only validation.
9. It must remain non-orchestrating and narrower than a conditional behavioral pattern.

### Disqualifiers

- It must not be merely descriptive metadata.
- It must not encode a multi-stage workflow.
- It must not depend on undeclared ambient state.
- It must not collapse multiple unrelated properties into one trait.

### Substantiation proof

The property is observable and testable across more than one valid context, and no single mote alone guarantees it.

A valid trait may become a constituent of a quirk; it does not become one merely by being placed beneath a `quirks/` path.

## 4. Quirk

**Constitutive essence:** A bounded conditional behavioral pattern formed from multiple traits, distinctive in when or how it manifests.

**Emergent property:** Behavior

**Canonical scope:** One trigger-governed behavior, strategy, policy reaction, adaptation, exception pattern, or specialized mode of response.

**Direct composition:** two or more distinct active trait instances.

### Required criteria

1. It must compose at least two distinct active trait instances.
2. It must declare trigger, guard, context, behavior, termination, and fallback.
3. It must produce a behavior distinguishable from each constituent trait.
4. It must remain bounded to one behavioral concern.
5. It must declare state requirements and side-effect boundaries.
6. It must be replayable or explicitly classify irreproducible external effects.
7. It must expose deterministic precedence when multiple quirks are simultaneously eligible.
8. It must preserve the traits as reusable references.
9. It must support focused behavioral tests including non-trigger cases.

### Disqualifiers

- It must not be an entire autonomous capability.
- It must not silently override sibling quirks.
- It must not own unrelated persistence, scheduling, and communication concerns.
- It must not treat novelty alone as qualification.

### Substantiation proof

Given the same authoritative context and trigger, the quirk selects and executes the same bounded behavior, while disabling it leaves the parent capability otherwise structurally intact.

A valid quirk may become a constituent of a prodigal; it does not become one merely by being placed beneath a `prodigals/` path.

## 5. Prodigal

**Constitutive essence:** An autonomous reusable capability assembled from multiple quirks and governed by a stable public contract.

**Emergent property:** Capability

**Canonical scope:** One independently invocable job, engine, subsystem capability, workflow, processor, planner, compiler, resolver, or service-like function.

**Direct composition:** two or more distinct active quirk instances.

### Required criteria

1. It must compose at least two distinct active quirk instances.
2. It must own one coherent capability contract with declared inputs, outputs, errors, and compatibility.
3. It must coordinate its quirks without absorbing their independent identity.
4. It must be independently invocable, testable, replaceable by compatible succession, and reusable.
5. It must declare operational state, resources, dependencies, and failure containment.
6. It must own its Rubric when it has executable implementation.
7. It must emit receipts for durable actions.
8. It must expose extension points through slots, attachments, moods, adapters, or typed Segues.
9. It must remain subordinate to exactly one primary Exile semantic owner while permitting Kindred relations elsewhere.

### Disqualifiers

- It must not claim broad domain authority.
- It must not span unrelated capabilities.
- It must not hide consumer-specific coupling behind a generic name.
- It must not be promoted solely because it has many files.

### Substantiation proof

It can be invoked through its contract as a complete capability, and its removal disables one capability without erasing the parent Exile's identity or all of its responsibilities.

A valid prodigal may become a constituent of an exile; it does not become one merely by being placed beneath an `exiles/` path.

## 6. Exile

**Constitutive essence:** A domain-responsible semantic owner composed of multiple Prodigals and accountable for one enduring operational concern.

**Emergent property:** Agency

**Canonical scope:** One named owner of a durable concern such as orchestration, evidence, mutation, contracts, composition, normalization, decomposition, conversation, or communication.

**Direct composition:** two or more distinct active prodigal instances.

### Required criteria

1. It must compose at least two distinct active prodigal instances.
2. It must own one durable semantic jurisdiction that remains meaningful independent of implementation.
3. It must define explicit boundaries, responsibilities, non-responsibilities, and collaboration contracts.
4. It must possess stable identity, authority, lineage, provenance, dependencies, dependents, and compatibility.
5. It must own all Rubrics whose primary meaning belongs to its jurisdiction.
6. It must consume Cabals only where shared qualification is proven.
7. It must coordinate Prodigals without duplicating their capability substance.
8. It must participate in Kindred through typed relations rather than informal coupling.
9. It must remain composable into an Innate without becoming a global root or universal utility.

### Disqualifiers

- It must not be a folder, namespace, team label, or arbitrary collection.
- It must not own unrelated domains.
- It must not contain generic shared code merely for convenience.
- It must not derive ownership from filename or path.

### Substantiation proof

Its jurisdiction can be stated as one enduring responsibility; every owned Prodigal materially serves that responsibility; moving a Prodigal elsewhere requires an explicit authority and dependency rationale.

A valid exile may become a constituent of an innate; it does not become one merely by being placed beneath an `innates/` path.

## 7. Innate

**Constitutive essence:** A constitutional faculty composed of multiple Exiles whose coordinated jurisdictions provide one intrinsic system ability.

**Emergent property:** Faculty

**Canonical scope:** One fundamental capability family that the containing Portal inherently possesses.

**Direct composition:** two or more distinct active exile instances.

### Required criteria

1. It must compose at least two distinct active exile instances.
2. It must express one intrinsic faculty broader than any constituent Exile jurisdiction.
3. It must define the constitutional guarantees the faculty provides to its Portal.
4. It must govern collaboration and conflict resolution among member Exiles.
5. It must declare admission criteria for Exiles and reject merely adjacent concerns.
6. It must expose a stable faculty contract to the enclosing Portal.
7. It must preserve Exile autonomy and ownership.
8. It must provide fault-containment boundaries preventing one Exile failure from silently corrupting siblings.
9. It must remain independent of any one external entry channel.

### Disqualifiers

- It must not be a miscellaneous category.
- It must not directly absorb Prodigals except through an explicitly modeled compatibility bridge.
- It must not represent an external boundary or entry surface.
- It must not duplicate Exile Rubrics.

### Substantiation proof

The faculty remains conceptually valid when any one Exile is replaced by a compatible successor, but ceases to be substantiated if it lacks plural complementary jurisdictions.

A valid innate may become a constituent of a portal; it does not become one merely by being placed beneath a `portals/` path.

## 8. Portal

**Constitutive essence:** A governed interaction boundary composed of multiple Innates, through which capabilities are admitted, exposed, translated, or traversed.

**Emergent property:** Access

**Canonical scope:** One coherent ingress, egress, mediation, protocol, audience, environment, or interaction boundary.

**Direct composition:** two or more distinct active innate instances.

### Required criteria

1. It must compose at least two distinct active innate instances.
2. It must define who or what may cross, in which direction, under which authority, contract, and policy.
3. It must mediate rather than duplicate the faculties of its Innates.
4. It must declare protocols, adapters, security boundaries, compatibility, rate or capacity constraints, and failure behavior.
5. It must provide typed Segues inward to Innates and outward to the Obelisk environment.
6. It must preserve end-to-end lineage and provenance for traversing payloads.
7. It must support denial by default at controlled capability boundaries.
8. It must remain independently observable and testable as a boundary.
9. It must avoid becoming a programming gate; historical identity Gate paths remain compatibility surfaces only.

### Disqualifiers

- It must not be a generic directory named gateway or interface.
- It must not own intrinsic faculty substance.
- It must not permit untyped traversal.
- It must not erase source identity during translation.

### Substantiation proof

A traversal can be validated from admission through mediation to result, with authority and provenance intact, and the Portal remains a boundary rather than a capability owner.

A valid portal may become a constituent of an obelisk; it does not become one merely by being placed beneath an `obelisks/` path.

## 9. Obelisk

**Constitutive essence:** A sovereign fundamental Savant system composed of multiple Portals and responsible for one complete, durable, top-level purpose.

**Emergent property:** Sovereignty

**Canonical scope:** One major foundational system with its own constitutional purpose, governed boundaries, lifecycle, assurance, and evolution.

**Direct composition:** two or more distinct active portal instances.

### Required criteria

1. It must compose at least two distinct active portal instances.
2. It must declare one complete top-level purpose that cannot be reduced to a single Portal.
3. It must govern its Portals through explicit authority, constitutional invariants, and compatibility law.
4. It must expose stable identity, lifecycle, lineage, provenance, dependencies, dependents, and recovery model.
5. It must contain all lower identity tiers through recursive composition without duplicating their substance.
6. It must provide system-level assurance, observability, replay, migration, and succession criteria.
7. It must remain independently extensible through new Portals and typed Segues.
8. It must coordinate with other Obelisks through explicit Kindred, Cabal, contract, or Portal relations.
9. It must avoid claiming Savant-wide authority unless explicitly established by higher constitutional authority.

### Disqualifiers

- It must not be a monolith.
- It must not flatten lower tiers.
- It must not serve as a dumping ground for unrelated systems.
- It must not be created because a project is large.

### Substantiation proof

Its purpose, boundaries, Portals, invariants, and failure/recovery model form a coherent sovereign system; removing one Portal degrades access but does not erase the Obelisk's identity.

## Criterion moods

The criteria are modularized as a nine-stage Modus-owned criterion pipeline. Moods remain operators, not hierarchy levels or physical containers.

- **anima:** activates identity and verifies that an element is a real instance rather than a path, file, projection, or placeholder.
- **weld:** binds distinct child instances into a parent composition while preserving their identities and references.
- **kiln:** hardens the composition through invariants, validation, replay, regression, and failure testing.
- **graft:** attaches optional capabilities, slots, adapters, and extensions without copying or replacing canonical substance.
- **aria:** projects and expresses the element through human, machine, diagnostic, and runtime views without creating authority.
- **mantle:** applies jurisdiction, authority, policy, security, custody, and responsibility boundaries.
- **fulcrum:** balances cardinality, cohesion, coupling, symmetry, capacity, and dependency direction.
- **echelon:** proves that emergence has crossed the threshold into the next distinct tier and prevents false promotion.
- **ascent:** compiles validated lower-tier composition into higher-order operation and, at runtime, prepares it for deferred shardization.

A complete substantiation evaluation applies all nine operators. Individual criteria may bind a subset during local checks, but promotion to a higher tier requires the complete pipeline.

## Digital scaffolding and conduits

The hierarchy is a recursive identity scaffold:

- **Nodes** are stable instances.
- **Vertical members** are direct composition edges between adjacent tiers.
- **Horizontal members** are typed Kindred, authority, dependency, provenance, compatibility, and Cabal relations.
- **Roads** are Segues. A Segue joins adjacent tiers, governs admissible transitions, and may provide contracts, adapters, policies, projections, and transition-scope Cabals without duplicating endpoint substance.
- **Travel units** are typed transit envelopes. They carry references rather than copied canonical substance.

A transit envelope contains identity, source, destination, payload reference, contract, authority witness, lineage, provenance, dependency and compatibility context, mood and slot bindings, validity, expiry, and receipt policy. The envelope allows work, evidence, commands, projections, or runtime material to traverse the scaffold without losing where it came from or what authorizes it.

## Runtime shardization

The user has established that runtime operation will automatically transform relevant material into shards. The exact shard semantics are intentionally deferred.

- Runtime shards are not the superseded second identity level.
- Canonical instances remain authoritative and are not replaced by shards.
- Shards are runtime projections or execution units derived deterministically from canonical instances.
- Every shard must retain source identity, digest, lineage, provenance, authority witness, and replay information.
- Shard format, granularity, lifecycle, routing, recombination, and persistence remain unresolved pending a later accepted decision.

This distinction is mandatory because `shard` was superseded by `mote` only as the second identity level; runtime shards remain a separate, still-unresolved concept.

## Dependency audit

No new dependency is required to record or validate the initial criteria. The smallest correct baseline is JSON plus a standard-library validator.

Recommended optional additions after authority acceptance:

- **CUE:** best fit for authoritative constraints, unification, composition, and validation. It should validate Savant semantics, never become their authority.
- **JSON Schema 2020-12:** portable validation and interchange projection.
- **Hypothesis:** property-based tests for invariants such as adjacency, cardinality, non-duplication, promotion, and deterministic round trips.
- **NetworkX:** optional diagnostic graph traversal, cycle detection, topological checks, ancestry, and dependency analysis. Core semantics should remain independent of it.

A database, graph server, ontology framework, workflow engine, or orchestration platform is not justified by this bounded task.

## Acceptance and migration consequences

This specification does not authorize movement. Once accepted, it should supersede weaker or incomplete tier definitions through a new accepted decision. The structure-intelligence scanner and destination planner must then classify every candidate using these criteria. Any artifact whose tier, owner, constituents, emergence proof, or authority remains unresolved must not move.

The future migration planner must prove, for every instance:

1. stable identity;
2. exact tier;
3. qualifying direct constituents;
4. emergence proof;
5. semantic and Rubric ownership;
6. Kindred, dependency, authority, provenance, and compatibility edges;
7. canonical destination;
8. reference rewrites and compatibility staging;
9. rollback and validation contract.

Only then may reversible physical restructuring begin.
