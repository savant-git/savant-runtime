# LEXICON CONSTITUTION

Every constitutional concept owns exactly one canonical lexeme.

Every canonical lexeme owns exactly one constitutional concept.

Canonical lexemes are immutable.

Aliases may exist.

Aliases never become canonical.

A superseded lexeme remains permanently reserved.

No canonical lexeme may be reused.

No subsystem may introduce terminology outside the lexicon.

Every script, shard, instance, segue, validator, projection, registry, and document references lexemes by identity rather than by duplicated definition.
