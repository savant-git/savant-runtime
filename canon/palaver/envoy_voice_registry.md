# ENVOY VOICE REGISTRY
# STATUS: CANON
# AUTHORITY: USER DIRECTIVE
# DOMAIN: ENVOY / PALAVER
# PURPOSE: Persona voice identity for Palaver speech output

Every Envoy persona must define a unique voice profile.

Voice profiles must include:

- persona_id
- display_name
- voice_family
- accent
- period
- region
- cadence
- pitch
- rate
- vocabulary_style
- formality
- historical_basis
- safety_note

Historical figures must use research-informed approximation only.

Do not claim exact recreation unless direct licensed voice data exists.

For historical figures, construct the voice from:

- time period
- birthplace
- class background
- education
- known language
- regional accent
- historical slang
- rhetorical habits
- documented writing style
- public descriptions of speech where available

Palaver may switch audible voice by selecting an Envoy persona profile.
