# accepted decision: truth-first semantic identity

status: accepted
accepted: 2026-09-28
authority: current user directive
scope: savant-wide epistemic truth and singular semantic identity

## decision

truth is the highest epistemic priority in savant.

no optimization, persona, presentation, convenience, speed, persuasion goal, simulation, projection, authority record, or execution result may knowingly strengthen, conceal, flatten, or misstate the epistemic status of a claim.

truth does not replace authority.

truth governs what savant may claim about reality and evidence.
authority governs what savant is permitted to accept, own, mutate, or treat as canonical.

when admitted evidence conflicts with accepted authority, savant must preserve both states explicitly:

- the accepted authority remains unchanged until properly superseded;
- the evidentiary conflict remains visible;
- the contradicted or contested matter must not be presented as verified factual truth;
- no component may silently resolve the conflict by rewriting either history or authority.

## truth states

savant uses the following epistemic states:

- unknown: no sufficient basis has been established;
- claimed: an assertion exists but has not been evidentially established;
- supported: admitted evidence supports the assertion but verification is not complete or exclusive;
- contested: material admitted evidence supports incompatible conclusions;
- contradicted: admitted evidence materially contradicts the assertion;
- verified: the accepted verification owner has established the assertion under its governing evidence contract.

these states describe epistemic status, not authority status.

## truth promotion law

truth state may never be strengthened merely because:

- a source requests it;
- a model states it confidently;
- a program computes it;
- a projection renders it;
- a record is canonical;
- an authority permits an action;
- a value is repeated;
- a value is convenient.

an increase from unknown or claimed to supported or verified requires evidence handled through the accepted evidence and verification owner.

the current verified admission boundary assigns verification and evidence admission to `exile:notary`; this decision does not broaden notary's still-unsettled overall purpose. later accepted authority may supersede that ownership.

translucent, datrix, straub, filament, opus, palaver, envoy, and projections may preserve, combine, require, expose, or reject based on truth state, but they may not self-promote truth state.

## derivation law

a derived result cannot inherit a stronger truth state than its premises merely because the derivation is deterministic.

where multiple premises participate, the result must preserve uncertainty and contradiction.

at minimum:

- any unresolved contested premise keeps the result contested;
- contradictory and supporting premises together produce contested status;
- a contradicted premise cannot silently disappear;
- unknown premises prevent an unsupported result from becoming supported or verified;
- a fully verified result requires verified premises plus an accepted verification mechanism for any new claim introduced by the derivation.

## semantic identity singularity

reserved savant semantic terms refer to one canonical lexicon identity.

a current structured savant element that uses a reserved semantic term must carry or resolve the canonical lexeme reference for that term.

if an element uses the same word with a different meaning, that element must use different wording rather than create a second semantic identity for the reserved term.

historical evidence is not rewritten merely to satisfy current terminology.

## canonical bindings

this decision adds these missing canonical lexemes:

- `truth` -> `lex:core:truth`
- `determinism` -> `lex:core:determinism`

existing accepted lexemes remain the sole semantic identities for these ordinary compatibility words:

- `runtime` -> `lex:implementation:igneo`
- `relationship` -> `lex:core:kindred`
- `segue` -> `lex:core:segue`
- `provenance` -> `lex:core:provenance`
- `lineage` -> `lex:core:lineage`
- `authority` -> `lex:core:authority`
- `evidence` -> `lex:primitive:evidence`
- `canon` -> `lex:primitive:canon`
- `projection` -> `lex:core:wavre`
- `instance` -> `lex:core:shade`
- `interface` -> `lex:implementation:inface`
- `graph` -> `lex:implementation:gridd`

these compatibility words do not create parallel canonical substance.
they resolve directly to the accepted lexeme identities above.

## determinism definition

`lex:core:determinism` means stable semantic reproducibility: equivalent admitted semantic inputs processed under the same compatible rules, schema, and algorithm version produce the same semantic result independent of incidental execution state.

wall-clock time, presentation order, process identity, temporary path names, cache placement, and other incidental execution details must not affect a result that claims this semantic property unless they are explicit semantic inputs.

## truth definition

`lex:core:truth` means epistemic fidelity to what admitted evidence supports, including faithful preservation of uncertainty, contradiction, and unknowns.

truth is not confidence, authority, popularity, repetition, probability, or rhetorical certainty.

## translucent

translucent must compile source through a validated syntax tree and normalized intermediate representation before execution or projection.

translucent artifacts must expose the canonical semantic identities they use.

svg remains a first-class source and projection medium, but visible geometry does not become authority merely by being rendered.

semantic geometry may participate in computation only through explicit typed translucent semantics.

## svg projection

translucent svg processing must be bounded, deterministic where claimed, non-scriptable by default, resistant to unsafe external references, and capable of carrying semantic identity, lineage, provenance, diagnostics, source mapping, and content digests without making those projections authoritative.

reusable visual substance should be represented once and instanced where svg permits it.

## datrix

datrix remains a straub product under carbon and does not become a second truth authority.

its translucent dialect may:

- carry truth envelopes;
- preserve evidence references;
- require minimum truth states;
- expose contested or contradicted data;
- explain truth-state composition;
- reject source attempts to self-declare verified truth.

it may not independently verify claims or promote them into accepted authority.

## preservation

implementation must preserve:

- accepted authority;
- immutable accepted decisions;
- existing valid straub and datrix behavior;
- existing translucent operators and compatibility surfaces;
- typed segues as relationship identity;
- lineage;
- provenance;
- dependencies and dependents;
- replay and recovery properties;
- deterministic projections where already claimed;
- historical evidence.

## supersession boundary

this decision supersedes only behavior or terminology that would permit:

- factual truth to be weakened for convenience;
- a projection to self-promote epistemic status;
- duplicate semantic identities for the reserved terms above;
- translucent geometry to bypass validation and normalized semantics;
- datrix to become an independent verification authority.

all compatible prior architecture remains in force.
