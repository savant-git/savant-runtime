# balm healing

status: constitutional canon  
authority: project-owner-directed  
accepted: 2026-08-24  
owner: balm  
primitive: healing  
supersedes: shatter

## purpose

balm owns healing.

healing means restoration of damaged, degraded, inconsistent, incomplete, or
nonviable savant state toward a coherent viable state while preserving
authority, evidence, lineage, provenance, and recoverability.

balm replaces shatter as an exile.

historical shatter artifacts remain historical evidence and must not be
silently rewritten to pretend that balm always existed.

## healing doctrine

balm prefers:

repair over replacement.

recovery over destruction.

reconciliation over arbitrary selection.

preservation over erasure.

minimal sufficient intervention over broad mutation.

reversible intervention over irreversible intervention.

evidence-preserving restoration over history rewriting.

## healing lifecycle

a healing operation may progress through:

detect
-> characterize
-> bound
-> preserve
-> diagnose
-> propose
-> authorize
-> repair
-> verify
-> stabilize
-> close

not every healing operation requires every stage.

## damage classes

balm may address:

- corrupted artifacts;
- malformed structures;
- broken references;
- missing dependencies;
- inconsistent projections;
- partial writes;
- interrupted operations;
- degraded services;
- invalid caches;
- stale projections;
- replay divergence;
- lineage discontinuity;
- provenance discontinuity;
- dependency breakage;
- recoverable configuration failure;
- incomplete migration;
- state desynchronization;
- damaged indexes;
- recoverable data inconsistency;
- failed composition;
- partially completed mutations.

## preservation

before destructive repair, balm should preserve sufficient pre-repair state
for diagnosis, rollback, evidence, or reconstruction whenever practical.

## mutation boundary

balm determines healing intent and repair requirements.

coda owns material filesystem mutation.

balm therefore composes with coda rather than duplicating coda's mutation
engine.

## evidence boundary

notary owns evidence.

balm may request verification and consume evidence but must not declare its
own repair successful merely because it performed a mutation.

## canon boundary

lore owns canon.

balm may restore canonical structures from authoritative sources but may not
invent replacement canon to make an inconsistency disappear.

## healing receipts

a healing receipt should be able to expose:

- healing identity;
- subject;
- damage classification;
- detected state;
- preserved state;
- proposed intervention;
- authority;
- mutations requested;
- mutations completed;
- evidence;
- verification;
- resulting state;
- unresolved damage;
- rollback path;
- provenance;
- lineage.

## terminal states

a healing operation may terminate as:

- healed;
- partially_healed;
- stabilized;
- quarantined;
- deferred;
- blocked;
- irrecoverable;
- superseded.

failure to heal must remain representable.

balm must never manufacture a successful result to satisfy a desired terminal
state.
