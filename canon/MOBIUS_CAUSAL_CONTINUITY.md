## purpose

mobius owns causal continuity.

mobius maintains the relationship between:

- what is presently true;
- what could happen;
- what decisions are available;
- what conditions constrain those decisions;
- what actually happens;
- why it happens;
- what changes because it happened;
- what becomes possible or impossible afterward;
- what secondary and remote consequences emerge;
- and what would have happened along alternatives that were not realized.

mobius is savant's continuity, causality, timeline, logistics, consequence,
decision-resolution, and butterfly-effect engine.

## possibility manifolds

mobius may preserve multiple mutually exclusive future continuations without
prematurely selecting one.

example:

present
├── a
├── b
├── c
└── d

the collection is an unresolved possibility manifold.

each branch may carry:

- branch identity;
- parent identity;
- divergence event;
- assumptions;
- evidence dependencies;
- canonical dependencies;
- constraints;
- required resources;
- logistical requirements;
- causal ancestry;
- projected consequences;
- uncertainty;
- weights where justified;
- simulation references;
- confidence;
- feasibility;
- temporal bounds;
- spatial bounds;
- resolution conditions;
- provenance;
- lineage.

a possibility manifold is not automatically exhaustive.

mobius must distinguish:

- enumerated possibilities;
- generated possibilities;
- sampled possibilities;
- inferred possibilities;
- pruned possibilities;
- impossible possibilities;
- unknown possibilities;
- computationally unexplored possibilities.

## causal collapse

when an unresolved decision becomes resolved, mobius performs causal collapse.

before:

{a | b | c | d}

after:

c

c becomes the realized causal continuation.

the alternatives remain preserved.

they become counterfactual continuations rather than realized continuations.

collapse therefore changes causal status rather than destroying information.

## resolution horizon

a decision may have a finite period during which resolution remains possible.

a resolution horizon may contain:

- opened_at;
- resolve_by;
- resolver;
- resolution policy;
- authority source;
- delegation scope;
- objective;
- constraints;
- forbidden outcomes;
- minimum confidence;
- fallback;
- expiry behavior;
- external conditions.

## resolution policies

### user_required

only the user may resolve the decision.

expiration does not authorize savant to act.

### delegate_on_expiry

the user retains first authority.

after the defined deadline, savant may resolve the decision only within an
explicitly granted delegation scope.

### default_on_expiry

an already-authorized default continuation becomes realized when the
resolution horizon expires.

### vanish_on_expiry

the decision opportunity disappears.

no candidate decision is promoted merely because the opportunity expired.

### external_resolution

an external event determines the realized continuation.

### first_condition

the first authorized resolution condition to become true determines the
continuation.

## delegation invariant

time does not create authority.

savant may never infer:

deadline expired -> savant may decide

unless an applicable authority explicitly establishes that transition.

delegated decisions should preserve:

- delegator;
- delegate;
- authority source;
- scope;
- deadline;
- objective;
- constraints;
- forbidden outcomes;
- confidence threshold;
- fallback;
- candidates considered;
- selected candidate;
- selection basis;
- resolution receipt.

## butterfly propagation

mobius must support consequence propagation beyond immediate effects.

decision
-> immediate consequence
-> changed condition
-> altered opportunity
-> participant reaction
-> secondary consequence
-> feedback
-> divergence
-> remote consequence

the propagation depth must be bounded by explicit computational or semantic
limits rather than pretending an infinite causal future can be exhaustively
enumerated.

## carbon composition

mobius does not replace carbon.

mobius supplies causal structures.

carbon executes simulations over those structures.

a mobius branch may reference one or many carbon simulations.

a carbon simulation result may produce new mobius branches.

neither operation transfers authority.

## zero composition

zero supplies quantitative machinery for:

- probabilities;
- distributions;
- combinatorics;
- optimization;
- uncertainty;
- state-space mathematics;
- sensitivity;
- quantitative branch comparison.

mobius owns the causal interpretation of the resulting structures.

## lore composition

lore supplies canonical starting state.

mobius may branch from canon without modifying canon.

a projected future is not canon.

a counterfactual past is not canon.

a realized event becomes eligible for canonical treatment only through the
applicable authority process.

## notary composition

notary distinguishes admitted evidence from unsupported assumptions.

mobius must preserve that distinction through causal propagation.

a consequence derived from an assumption must not silently acquire the
evidentiary status of an observed fact.

## quantum analogy

mobius possibility manifolds deliberately permit multiple mutually exclusive
future states to remain unresolved simultaneously.

this may be described as superposition-like behavior at the architectural
level.

it is not a claim that ordinary savant decisions are quantum physical
superpositions.

actual quantum mathematics belongs to zero.

actual physical quantum modeling may be consumed by cataxis and carbon where
appropriate.

## replay

mobius causal structures should be replayable from their authoritative or
admitted primitives wherever practical.

a replay should preserve:

- starting state references;
- decision state;
- branches;
- causal edges;
- resolution conditions;
- collapse event;
- realized branch;
- counterfactual branches;
- evidence references;
- simulation references;
- provenance;
- lineage.
