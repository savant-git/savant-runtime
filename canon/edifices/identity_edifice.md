===============================================================================
IDENTITY edifice
===============================================================================

Purpose:

Answers:

    What kind of thing is this?

This edifice describes identity, composition, responsibility,
and conceptual scope.

Every level is composed of one or more instances of the level below it.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

IOTA

Definition:

The smallest meaningful primitive.

Criteria:

- cannot be meaningfully decomposed
- represents a single atomic concept
- has one responsibility
- exists primarily as a building block

Examples:

- a single symbolic primitive
- a single capability primitive
- a single semantic atom

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

MOTE

Definition:

A small coherent collection of iotas.

Criteria:

- composed of multiple iotas
- forms a recognizable pattern
- still narrowly scoped
- reusable across systems

Examples:

- a recurring semantic pattern
- a recurring behavioral pattern

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

TRAIT

Definition:

A persistent characteristic.

Criteria:

- composed of multiple motes
- describes what something tends to be
- expresses identity rather than behavior
- remains relatively stable over time

Examples:

- persistence
- curiosity
- precision
- adaptability

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

QUIRK

Definition:

A single transform.

Criteria:

- composed of multiple traits
- performs one distinct operation
- has one primary purpose
- cannot reasonably be split into multiple independent behaviors

Examples:

- normalize
- classify
- verify
- route
- rank

Rule:

A quirk should do one thing.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

PRODIGAL

Definition:

A capability.

Criteria:

- composed of multiple quirks
- solves a coherent class of problems
- exposes a recognizable capability
- may internally orchestrate many transforms

Examples:

- Fortex
- persona synthesis
- recursive analysis
- topology compilation

Rule:

A prodigal owns a capability.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

EXILE

Definition:

A domain.

Criteria:

- composed of multiple prodigals
- owns a conceptual territory
- governs a distinct category of work
- can operate largely independently

Examples:

- Carbon
- Cataxis
- Coda
- Envoy
- Filament
- Graffiti
- Lore
- Mobius
- Modus
- Niche
- Notary
- Opus
- Pact
- Palaver
- Shatter
- Underscore
- Urge
- Zero

Rule:

An exile owns a domain.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

INNATE

Definition:

A family of domains.

Criteria:

- composed of multiple exiles
- governs a larger functional region
- defines a major branch of capability
- establishes shared identity across domains

Rule:

An innate owns multiple related domains.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

GATE

Definition:

A major ontology region.

Criteria:

- composed of multiple innates
- separates large regions of the ontology
- establishes major organizational boundaries
- governs broad categories of existence

Rule:

A gate owns multiple innates.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

OBELISK

Definition:

The highest identity structure.

Criteria:

- composed of multiple gates
- represents a complete ontology
- defines the largest coherent organizational structure

Rule:

An obelisk owns multiple gates.

===============================================================================
CANONICAL PRINCIPLE
===============================================================================

Each edifice answers a different question:

Identity:

    What is it?

Scale:

    How large is it?

Content:

    How much content does it contain?

An object may possess all three classifications simultaneously.

Example:

    Palaver

    Identity:
        Exile

    Scale:
        Body

    Content:
        System
