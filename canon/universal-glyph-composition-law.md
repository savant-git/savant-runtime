# universal glyph composition law

status: accepted constitutional architecture
authority: current user directive
accepted: 2026-09-10
scope: all savant-owned symbolic composition

## law

savant has one universal shard 00.

shard 00 is the atomic reality of savant-owned symbolic substance.

every glyph is substantiated once in shard 00.

nothing above shard 00 duplicates glyph substance.

all higher symbolic structures are compositions of instances.

the canonical operation is:

substantiate once
→ instance
→ compose
→ relate through typed segues
→ project

## universal edifice

glyph
→ glyph instance
→ glyph-group instance
→ glyph-group composition
→ recursively higher composition
→ deterministic projection

the edifice is recursive.

a higher composition contains references to lower compositions.

it does not acquire duplicate ownership of their substance.

## atomic identity

each distinct glyph has one canonical atomic identity.

glyph identity and glyph occurrence are separate.

the same glyph may occur any number of times through instances without being resubstantiated.

an occurrence therefore references atomic substance and supplies occurrence-specific structure.

## instance law

an instance may carry:

- reference
- position
- role
- attachment
- provenance
- lineage
- relationship
- parameterization
- projection metadata

an instance must not duplicate the canonical substance it references.

## composition law

every non-atomic symbolic artifact is a composition.

compositions may contain:

- glyph instances
- composition instances
- typed segue instances

compositions may recursively compose compositions without an artificial maximum depth.

## identity separation

textual identity and semantic identity are distinct.

two compositions may materialize identical text while retaining distinct semantic identity, provenance, authority, or lineage.

structurally identical immutable compositions may share content-addressed structural identity when their authority permits it.

semantic identity must never be inferred solely from byte equality.

## typed segue law

relationships belong in typed segues.

a relationship must not be encoded by duplicating the substance of either endpoint.

a typed segue exposes at minimum:

- identity
- type
- source
- target
- authority
- lineage
- provenance

additional parameters may be attached without changing endpoint substance.

## mutation law

immutable lower-level substance is never destructively rewritten to represent higher-level mutation.

mutation produces a successor composition.

successor compositions preserve predecessor lineage.

shared immutable lower-level compositions remain reusable.

## projection law

conventional source files, documents, manifests, commands, and other textual artifacts may be deterministic materializations of composition authority.

where the universal composition substrate is authoritative:

composition
→ deterministic projection
→ conventional compatibility artifact

a projected artifact must be reproducible from its authoritative composition and projection contract.

## ingestion law

legacy textual artifacts may be admitted through deterministic decomposition.

the migration sequence is:

existing artifact
→ exact byte-preserving observation
→ glyph decomposition
→ glyph instances
→ recursive compositions
→ projection
→ equality verification
→ authority transition only when explicitly admitted

filesystem presence alone never establishes authority.

migration must preserve, where applicable:

- bytes
- encoding
- ordering
- executable meaning
- identity
- lineage
- provenance
- authority
- dependencies
- dependents
- compatibility

## dependency law

higher structures expose dependencies on the compositions they reference.

dependencies and dependents must remain discoverable.

composition must not conceal coupling.

## provenance law

every authoritative composition must expose sufficient provenance to determine where its substance and structure originated.

projection must not erase provenance.

## lineage law

successor structures preserve ancestry.

replacement does not silently destroy history.

supersession is explicit.

accepted decisions remain immutable.

## authority law

composition does not create authority.

projection does not create authority.

reuse does not transfer authority.

instance creation does not transfer authority.

typed segues do not transfer authority unless a higher accepted contract explicitly establishes such transfer.

## determinism law

given the same:

- atomic registry
- composition graph
- parameters
- segue graph
- projection contract
- authoritative inputs

savant must produce the same deterministic projection wherever deterministic behavior is required.

## reversibility law

migration into universal composition must be reversible until the new authority is explicitly accepted.

projection must permit reconstruction of compatibility artifacts without manual rewriting.

## noumenon compliance

noumenon is subject to this law.

noumenon must not create a private glyph registry or competing atomic edifice.

noumenon schemas, contracts, state projections, receipts, commands, and executable source material must use the universal composition substrate wherever savant's glyph substrate governs symbolic construction.

## numinon compliance

numinon binds systems.

numinon does not duplicate their substance.

cross-system composition uses references and typed segues.

numinon must preserve endpoint identity, authority, lineage, provenance, dependencies, and dependents.

## universal invariant

there is one atomic symbolic substrate.

there may be indefinitely many instances.

there may be indefinitely many recursive compositions.

there may be indefinitely many typed relationships.

there must not be competing substantiations of the same canonical atomic substance.
