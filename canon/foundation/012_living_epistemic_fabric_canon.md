# savant living epistemic fabric canon

## status

This canon establishes the architectural destination and invariants for
Savant's living epistemic fabric. It does not claim that every
capability described here is already implemented.

## purpose

Savant's living epistemic fabric is the distributed capability by which
Savant acquires, admits, relates, maintains, challenges, refreshes,
projects, and applies knowledge without creating a parallel truth
authority.

It is not a monolithic knowledge engine.

It emerges from existing authoritative primitives, instances, typed
segues, semantic identity, relationship authority, evidence admission,
provenance, lineage, temporality, contradiction preservation,
uncertainty representation, deterministic projection, and bounded
acquisition.

## governing rule

External information becomes neither truth nor authority merely because
Savant retrieved, indexed, embedded, summarized, repeated, simulated, or
generated it.

The canonical flow is:

external world → bounded acquisition → source observation → provenance,
temporality, and integrity → evidence admission → atomic knowledge
primitives → typed segues → established semantic, relational, and
verification owners → living epistemic graph → deterministic
consumer-specific projections.

## invariants

1.  authority cannot be manufactured;
2.  evidence cannot be silently discarded;
3.  unknown is not false;
4.  inference is not fact;
5.  provider agreement is not truth;
6.  popularity is not authority;
7.  persona cannot rewrite evidence;
8.  simulated affect cannot rewrite evidence;
9.  derived state is not primitive authority;
10. summaries do not replace sources;
11. newer information does not automatically supersede higher authority;
12. contradiction must remain recoverable;
13. provenance must survive normalization;
14. historical truth must not be rewritten by current truth;
15. self-generated output cannot validate itself;
16. external retrieval cannot bypass admission;
17. speculative discovery remains speculative until admitted;
18. derived indexes must not become hidden truth systems;
19. consumer-specific projections must not mutate shared knowledge;
20. absence of evidence must not silently become evidence of absence.

## atomic knowledge

Knowledge should be substantiated at the smallest semantically
meaningful reusable granularity justified by the source.

Atomic knowledge remains identifiable, graph-addressable,
provenance-aware, lineage-aware, temporally situated where applicable,
dependency-aware, reusable, composable, attachable, extensible,
independently challengeable, independently supersedable, and
deterministically projectable.

Atomicity must not destroy source context.

## provenance and temporality

Claims should preserve useful relationships to source, source location,
observation, acquisition, normalization, evidence admission,
transformation lineage, succession lineage, and dependent projections.

Observation time, publication time, acquisition time, effective time,
expiration time, supersession time, retraction time, and historical
validity must remain distinguishable where materially relevant.

## contradiction and uncertainty

Contradiction is information and must not be silently erased.

Uncertainty is distinct from truth, authority, popularity, provider
agreement, and confidence.

Competing claims, hypotheses, theories, interpretations, and
counterfactuals remain explicitly typed according to their epistemic
status.

## lifecycle

Knowledge may require states including observed, candidate, evidenced,
admitted, contested, provisional, accepted, superseded, deprecated,
retracted, quarantined, and historical.

Existing accepted Savant lifecycle primitives must be reused when they
already carry equivalent meaning.

Transitions preserve history, lineage, provenance, and transition
authority.

## evidence topology

Evidence relationships may represent support, contradiction,
corroboration, dependency, derivation, replication, retraction,
supersession, observation, interpretation, and source dependence.

Repeated derivative reporting must not masquerade as independent
confirmation.

## deterministic projection

Consumer-specific knowledge views are derived projections.

Given the same admitted state, authority state, policy, and explicit
inputs, the same semantic projection should be reconstructable.

Indexes, embeddings, vector stores, caches, search databases, and
summaries are derived acceleration or compression structures unless
accepted authority establishes otherwise.

## ownership

The fabric composes existing owners.

It does not duplicate semantic identity, relationship authority,
evidence verification, memory ownership, authority, or accepted canon.

Opus consumes epistemic projections for cognition.

Carbon consumes admitted or explicitly hypothetical projections for
simulation.

Envoy consumes bounded projections for interaction and persona.

None may rewrite epistemic authority outside its established ownership
boundary.

## completion condition

Implementation is complete only when Savant can preserve source
substance and provenance, admit evidence through the proper boundary,
represent reusable knowledge, preserve contradiction and uncertainty,
maintain temporal succession, derive bounded task-specific projections,
invalidate stale dependents, rebuild derived state, produce receipts,
replay selection, and serve multiple consumers without duplicate
authority.
