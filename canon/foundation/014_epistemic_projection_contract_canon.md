# savant epistemic projection contract canon

## status

This canon defines the requirements for bounded consumer-specific
projections from the living epistemic fabric.

## purpose

Different Savant consumers require different knowledge without requiring
different truth systems.

An epistemic projection contract expresses what a consumer needs while
leaving shared knowledge unchanged.

## contract dimensions

A contract may specify:

-   required domains;
-   required authority floor;
-   maximum tolerated uncertainty;
-   temporal horizon;
-   historical depth;
-   contradiction inclusion;
-   provenance depth;
-   theoretical material allowance;
-   speculation allowance;
-   freshness requirement;
-   source-diversity requirement;
-   resource budget;
-   and consumer identity.

## projection law

A contract selects and shapes a derived projection.

It does not mutate shared knowledge.

It does not admit evidence.

It does not create authority.

It does not erase contradiction.

It does not upgrade hypotheses into facts.

## consumer examples

A production implementation consumer may require current accepted
authority, verified implementation, minimal inference, and exclusion of
speculation.

A scientific simulation consumer may require accepted findings,
supported hypotheses, leading competing theories, contradictions, and
deep provenance.

An Opus cognitive constellation may request exactly the knowledge
required by its active cognitive facets and no more.

An Envoy interaction projection may request psychology or interaction
knowledge while remaining unable to rewrite epistemic authority.

## receipts

Material projections should be capable of producing a deterministic
epistemic receipt containing, where applicable:

-   projection identity;
-   policy identity;
-   authority snapshot identity;
-   evidence references;
-   contradiction references;
-   uncertainty state;
-   provenance references;
-   lifecycle versions;
-   consumer identity;
-   deterministic selection reasons;
-   and semantic digest.

Receipts prove composition and selection history.

Receipts are not authority.

## replay

Given the same admitted knowledge state, authority state, policy,
contract, and deterministic inputs, the semantic projection should be
reconstructable.

Nondeterministic external acquisition becomes replayable only after its
observation has been preserved as input evidence.

## completion condition

The contract mechanism is complete only when consumers can obtain
bounded, deterministic, provenance-preserving, contradiction-aware
knowledge projections without mutating shared epistemic state or
creating duplicate authority.
