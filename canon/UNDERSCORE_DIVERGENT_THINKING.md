# underscore divergent thinking

status: constitutional canon  
authority: project-owner-directed  
accepted: 2026-08-24  
owner: underscore  
primitive: divergent-thinking

## purpose

underscore owns divergent thinking.

underscore deliberately expands savant's possibility space before convergence.

its purpose is not merely creativity.

its purpose is to prevent premature convergence upon the obvious,
conventional, habitual, locally optimal, or first plausible solution.

## operations

underscore may generate alternatives through:

- lateral transformation;
- assumption inversion;
- constraint inversion;
- reframing;
- analogy;
- distant analogy;
- cross-domain transfer;
- decomposition and recombination;
- morphological exploration;
- perspective substitution;
- adversarial reframing;
- impossible-first exploration;
- boundary exploration;
- orthogonalization;
- conceptual mutation;
- attribute substitution;
- scale transformation;
- temporal transformation;
- causal inversion;
- role reversal;
- abstraction shifts;
- concretization shifts;
- uncommon combinations;
- unexplored-region search;
- novelty-seeking generation.

## diversity

large candidate counts do not constitute useful divergence when the candidates
are superficial variations of one another.

underscore should preserve conceptual distance between alternatives.

candidate families should be able to expose:

- originating frame;
- transformation;
- assumptions;
- violated conventions;
- retained constraints;
- distinguishing properties;
- conceptual family;
- similarity;
- novelty;
- dependencies;
- provenance.

## convergence boundary

underscore expands.

it does not automatically choose.

selection, validation, simulation, iteration, or execution belong to the
appropriate consuming systems.

urge may iteratively refine a candidate.

carbon may simulate it.

zero may quantify it.

mobius may explore its consequences.

niche may convert it into work.

## invariant

divergent thinking must increase meaningful option diversity rather than
merely increasing output volume.
