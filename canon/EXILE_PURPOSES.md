# savant exile purposes

status: constitutional canon  
authority: project-owner-directed  
accepted: 2026-08-24  
scope: canonical singular purposes of savant's eighteen exiles

## 1. constitutional rule

the eighteen exiles are differentiated macro-engines.

each exile owns one irreducible operational purpose.

an exile may expose arbitrarily sophisticated capabilities, applications,
workers, projections, protocols, interfaces, and compositions, but those
capabilities must emerge from its singular purpose rather than turning the
exile into a generic duplicate of another exile.

capability overlap does not transfer ownership.

when multiple exiles participate in one operation, each retains ownership of
its own substance and the resulting higher behavior emerges through
composition and typed segues.

the canonical eighteen exiles are:

1. carbon
2. cataxis
3. coda
4. envoy
5. filament
6. graffiti
7. lore
8. mobius
9. modus
10. niche
11. notary
12. opus
13. pact
14. palaver
15. balm
16. underscore
17. urge
18. zero

shatter is superseded by balm.

segue is not a nineteenth exile. segue is savant's typed connective and
transitional primitive through which independently substantiated elements
compose.

## 9. mobius — causal continuity

singular purpose: causal continuity.

mobius owns the relationship between what could happen, what does happen,
why it happens, what follows from it, and what would have happened otherwise.

mobius governs continuity across decisions, events, timelines, dependencies,

mobius may own:

- causal graphs;

- timeline divergence;
- continuity;

- secondary consequences;

- decision branches;

- branch pruning;

- branch convergence;
- counterfactual continuity;
- realized histories;
- unrealized histories;
- continuation after interruption;
- causal replay;
- causal checkpoints;
- causal lineage;
- external resolution;
- delegated resolution;
- expiry behavior;
- opportunity disappearance.

### unresolved possibility state

before resolution, mutually exclusive futures may remain simultaneously
represented:

present -> {a | b | c | d}

this is a computational possibility manifold.

it is analogous to superposition as an architectural metaphor but does not
assert that ordinary savant decision analysis is quantum physics.

### causal collapse

when a final decision or external event resolves the possibility state:

{a | b | c | d} -> c

c becomes the realized continuation.

a, b, and d are not destroyed.

they remain preserved as unrealized counterfactual branches.

this permits later comparison between realized history and plausible
alternatives.

### resolution horizon

an unresolved decision may carry an explicit resolution policy.

supported conceptual policies include:

- user_required;
- delegate_on_expiry;
- default_on_expiry;
- vanish_on_expiry;
- external_resolution;
- first_condition.

### delegated resolution

savant may resolve a user's decision only when authority explicitly permits
it.

expiration alone does not create delegation.

delegated resolution must be able to represent:

- resolver;
- delegation scope;
- deadline;
- objective;
- constraints;
- forbidden outcomes;
- confidence requirement;
- fallback;
- authority source;
- resulting receipt.

mobius does not simulate every possible world itself.

carbon performs simulation.

mobius makes possible worlds causally continuous histories.

canonical primitive:

causal-continuity
