# SAVANT TRIADIC CARDINALITY CANON

Version: 1.0.0
Status: PROPOSED CANON
Date: 2026-08-07
Scope: Designed plural Savant structures

## 1. Governing law

Every deliberately designed plural Savant structure whose cardinality is under Savant's control MUST contain:

- exactly 3 members; or
- a positive multiple of 9 members.

Preferred cardinalities are:

3
9
18
27
36
45
54
63
72
81

Larger cardinalities remain legal when they are multiples of 9.

## 2. Recursive triadic organization

A multiple-of-nine collection SHOULD be organized recursively through semantic triads whenever that organization is coherent.

Preferred examples:

9 = 3 groups × 3 members

18 = 6 groups × 3 members

27 = 9 groups × 3 members

36 = 12 groups × 3 members

The numerical rule must reinforce semantic organization rather than produce meaningless padding.

## 3. Applicable structures

- execution pipelines;
- pipeline stages;
- pipeline phases;
- state models;
- capability families;
- ability families;
- validation dimensions;
- health dimensions;
- event taxonomies;
- projection families;
- interface surfaces;
- metadata contracts;
- Equalizer channels;

- other intentionally enumerable Savant architecture.

## 4. Exempt structures

The law MUST NOT falsify reality merely to satisfy cardinality.

Exempt structures include:

- actual populations of authoritative identities;
- externally defined enumerations;
- provider-defined enumerations;
- compatibility surfaces fixed by an external protocol;
- observed dependency populations;
- observed dependent populations;
- historical immutable records;
- naturally variable collections;
- query result populations;
- runtime-discovered collections;
- user-supplied collections;
- collections whose members arise from accepted ontology rather than design convenience.

An exemption preserves the real cardinality.

Fake members MUST NOT be created to numerically pad an exempt structure.

## 6. Exile cardinality

The current population of eighteen Exiles is cardinality-compliant.

Eighteen equals:

6 semantic triads

where such semantic grouping is later accepted.

The existence of eighteen Exiles does not authorize arbitrary grouping into triads without semantic authority.

## 7. Execution pipeline law

Every full Savant execution pipeline contains exactly eighteen canonical stage slots.

The universal shape is:

6 phases × 3 stages = 18 stages.

An operation may mark a stage:

- active;
- bypassed;
- inapplicable;

but MUST preserve the eighteen-slot pipeline identity for observability, replay, interoperability, and comparison.

A domain Focal supplies stage semantics.

It does not create a differently shaped pipeline framework.

## 8. Scyon application

Scyon successor contracts MUST normalize deliberately designed noncompliant enumerations toward 3, 9, 18, 27, 36, or another multiple of 9.

Historical accepted Scyon decisions remain immutable.

Normalization occurs through successor contracts and deterministic compatibility projections.

Existing working Scyon behavior MUST NOT be broken merely to achieve cardinality.

## 18. Compatibility law

Cardinality normalization MUST preserve:

- accepted authority;
- historical evidence;
- lineage;
- provenance;
- replay;
- current working behavior;
- compatibility;
- existing identifiers where possible.

A noncompliant historical contract is superseded or projected through a successor.

It is not destructively rewritten merely to normalize its count.

## 19. No numerical theater

The cardinality rule exists to produce:

- consistency;
- recursive composability;
- predictable interfaces;
- memorable structural grammar;
- stronger symmetry;
- easier validation;
- stable extension slots.

It MUST NOT produce arbitrary filler.

If only three legitimate members exist, use three.

If nine legitimate members do not exist, do not invent nine.

If a collection is naturally variable, exempt it.

## 20. Successor rule

Every newly created Lore, Living Canon, Scyon, Exile, Focal, and shared-ability contract SHALL be cardinality-compliant from inception.

Older noncompliant designed structures SHALL be normalized only when their successor is implemented or when the mismatch materially affects current operation.
