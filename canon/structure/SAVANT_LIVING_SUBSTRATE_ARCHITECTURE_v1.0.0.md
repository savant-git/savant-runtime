## 1. Purpose

Savant defines nine reusable living substrates.

They are universal implementation mechanisms.

They are not new identity tiers.

They are not Exiles.

They are not independent authority owners.

They do not supersede accepted operational ownership.

They may be instantiated repeatedly within their own domains and specialized by overlays, Focals, metadata, policies, bindings, masks, attachments, and other accepted Savant composition mechanisms.

## 2. Canonical living substrates

1. SCYON
2. SPLYCE
3. SCRYBE
4. PRYME
5. CYPHER
6. THRYCE
7. SPYRAL
8. LYTHE
9. DRYVE

## 3. Universal substrate law

Every living substrate follows:

SUBSTRATE
→ INSTANCE
→ SPECIALIZATION LAYER
→ CONCRETE LIVING IMPLEMENTATION

The substrate contains only behavior universal to its domain.

An instance supplies identity and composition context.

The specialization layer supplies purpose-specific configuration and metadata.

Canonical domain substance must not be duplicated into an instance or specialization layer.

Every substrate must remain:

- instanceable;
- overlay-driven;
- graph-addressable;
- composable;
- extensible;
- deterministic where applicable;
- authority-aware;
- lineage-preserving;
- provenance-preserving;
- replay-compatible;
- inspectable;
- independently testable.

## 4. Domain boundaries

### SCYON

Domain:

living structural substantiation.

Owns reusable mechanics for:

- structural identity;
- composition;
- attachment;
- metadata;
- event history;
- state;
- tier capacity;
- Focal specialization;
- living structural behavior.

Scyon does not replace the universal Savant instance model.

Scyon implements living behavior for eligible instances.

### SPLYCE

Domain:

living interface and interaction.

Owns reusable mechanics for:

- interface composition;
- controls;
- visualization;
- interactive graph surfaces;
- developer operation;
- runtime presentation;
- interface state.

Splyce does not own the authority or substance it displays.

### SCRYBE

Domain:

living memory and recall.

Owns reusable mechanics for:

- recall;
- temporal memory projection;
- context hydration;
- relevance ranking;
- memory traversal;
- memory explanation.

Scrybe does not own an independent canonical memory store.

Fluid Canon remains the canonical memory substance where established.

### PRYME

Domain:

living authority interpretation.

Owns reusable mechanics for:

- authority discovery;
- explicit precedence projection;
- authority-context assembly;
- conflict detection;
- supersession traversal;
- authority explanation.

Pryme does not manufacture authority.

Pryme does not accept decisions.

Pryme does not infer authority merely from filesystem location, filenames, confidence, state labels, or implementation presence.

Pryme may resolve authority only where accepted authority supplies sufficient explicit precedence and binding information.

Otherwise Pryme must preserve the conflict or return unresolved.

### CYPHER

Domain:

living interpretation and exchange.

Owns reusable mechanics for:

- translation;
- normalization;
- adapter selection;
- serialization;
- contract-preserving exchange;
- compatibility transformation.

Cypher does not own transport authority or translated substance.

### THRYCE

Domain:

living assurance.

Owns reusable mechanics for:

- validation;
- invariant evaluation;
- regression detection;
- health projection;
- integrity checking;
- assurance receipts.

Notary remains the accepted operational owner of verification, evidence admission, and attestation.

Thryce supplies reusable assurance mechanics to Notary and other authorized consumers.

### SPYRAL

Domain:

living evolution.

Owns reusable mechanics for:

- version transition;
- migration modeling;
- compatibility evolution;
- supersession;
- reversible adaptation;
- transition history.

Spyral does not authorize mutation.

Coda remains the accepted operational owner of authorized durable mutation.

### LYTHE

Domain:

living derivation and projection.

Owns reusable mechanics for:

- deterministic derivation;
- materialized views;
- projection specification;
- disposable representations;
- projection freshness;
- derivation explanation.

Filament remains the accepted operational owner of projection execution, projection runtime, and projection workers.

Lythe defines reusable derivation mechanics; Filament executes authorized projections.

### DRYVE

Domain:

living agency and execution mechanics.

Owns reusable mechanics for:

- intent representation;
- eligibility;
- execution planning;
- action lifecycle;
- outcome capture;
- compensation;
- execution replay.

Dryve does not own task authority or AI orchestration.

Niche remains the accepted owner of task discovery, decomposition, priority, scheduling, and task state.

Opus remains the accepted owner of AI provider routing, AI orchestration, and AI execution.

## 5. Existing operational ownership remains authoritative

The living substrate family must preserve accepted operational ownership, including:

- Niche — task discovery, decomposition, priority, scheduling, task state;
- Opus — AI provider routing, AI orchestration, AI execution;
- Notary — verification, evidence admission, attestation;
- Coda — authorized durable mutation;
- Pact — contracts, constraints, permissions, agreements;
- Modus — modular composition rules;
- Underscore — structural normalization and hidden support;
- Shatter — controlled decomposition and fault isolation;
- Palaver — conversational surfaces;
- Envoy — outward communication;
- Filament — projection execution, runtime, and workers.

A substrate may supply reusable mechanics to these owners.

It must not silently supersede them.

## 6. Authority boundary

Authority precedence remains:

1. current user directive;
2. accepted authoritative graph;
3. accepted decisions;
4. constitutional canon;
5. verified implementation;
6. admitted evidence;
7. deterministic projection;
8. historical implementation;
9. historical documents;
10. inference;
11. speculation.

A living substrate must not reorder this precedence.

Metadata is not automatically authority.

Filesystem presence is not authority.

Confidence is not authority.

Implementation maturity is not authority.

A projection is not authority unless separately accepted as such.

## 7. Composition law

Higher systems may compose any number of compatible living substrate instances.

Example:

an Exile may be:

- Scyon-substantiated;
- Splyce-presented;
- Scrybe-memory-enabled;
- Pryme-authority-aware;
- Cypher-connected;
- Thryce-assured;
- Spyral-evolvable;
- Lythe-projectable;
- Dryve-executable.

Composition does not merge jurisdiction.

Each substrate retains its domain boundary.

## 8. Instance law

Instances must reference canonical substance rather than copy it.

Each instance must expose or reference:

- stable identity;
- substrate identity;
- owner;
- specialization layer;
- authority state;
- lineage;
- provenance;
- dependencies;
- dependents where derived;
- capabilities;
- configuration;
- metadata;
- version;
- health;
- validation;
- history where applicable.

## 9. Cardinality

The living substrate family contains exactly nine canonical substrate types.

This ninefold count does not justify artificial features.

Internal cardinalities of 3, 9, 18, 27, 36, or other positive multiples of 9 are used only where the concepts are real and independently useful.

No placeholder feature may exist solely to satisfy symmetry.

## 10. Enterprise-grade requirement

Every canonical living substrate must undergo the same implementation discipline.

Each must include:

- at least 20 substantive enhancements;
- evaluation of useful contemporary libraries, dependencies, techniques, snippets, and public implementations;
- only justified dependencies;
- instance and specialization architecture;
- deterministic behavior where applicable;
- authority boundaries;
- lineage and provenance preservation;
- health;
- validation;
- observability;
- replay or reconstructability where applicable;
- compatibility preservation;
- one focused functional verification;
- one relevant integration or startup verification.

## 11. Non-overlap rule

The nine living substrates are distinguished by their verbs:

SCYON substantiates.

SPLYCE presents.

SCRYBE remembers.

PRYME interprets authority.

CYPHER translates.

THRYCE assures.

SPYRAL evolves.

LYTHE derives.

DRYVE acts.

If two substrates begin owning the same authoritative primitive, their implementation is incorrect and must be reconciled against accepted authority.

## 12. Migration boundary

This architecture authorizes extension and composition.

It does not authorize destructive physical migration.

Existing implementations remain in place unless separately authorized.

New living-substrate implementations must prefer:

- adapters;
- bindings;
- instances;
- overlays;
- Focals;
- projections;
- compatibility layers;
- reversible transitions.

over destructive replacement.
