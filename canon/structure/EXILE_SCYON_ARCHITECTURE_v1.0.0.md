## 2. FUNDAMENTAL SCYON CLASSIFICATION

Scyon is not an Exile.

Scyon is not a Prodigal.

Scyon is not a Quirk.

Scyon is not a Trait.

Scyon is not an identity tier.

Scyon is Savant's shared tier-scaled living operational organism.

A qualifying Savant identity is instantiated as a Scyon at the identity's tier.

Therefore:

- a Trait may be a Trait-tier Scyon;
- a Quirk may be a Quirk-tier Scyon;
- a Prodigal may be a Prodigal-tier Scyon;
- an Exile may be an Exile-tier Scyon;
- higher qualifying identity tiers may similarly possess their tier-scaled Scyon form.

Scyon itself remains the common organism.

The identity supplies identity.

The tier supplies tier bounds.

The Focal supplies specialization.

Authority supplies permission.

Composition supplies constituent structure.

Moods supply applicable operators.

State and resources supply current operational condition.

## 5. SINGLY SUBSTANTIATED, RECURSIVELY COMPOSED

Scyon is singly substantiated but recursively compositional.

A higher-tier Scyon may compose lower-tier identities that are themselves Scyon instances.

This does not mean embedding duplicate copies of the Scyon kernel.

The correct model is reference and composition.

Example:

Niche
= Exile-tier Scyon

Niche may compose:

Masterplan
= Prodigal-tier Scyon

Masterplan may compose:

Quirks
= Quirk-tier Scyons

Those Quirks may compose:

Traits
= Trait-tier Scyons

Every one of these identities uses the same shared Scyon substrate at its legitimate tier.

## 6. NO RECURSIVE KERNEL DUPLICATION

Forbidden architecture:

Exile Scyon
contains copied Prodigal Scyon kernel
contains copied Quirk Scyon kernel
contains copied Trait Scyon kernel

Required architecture:

one shared Scyon kernel
→ multiple tier-bounded instances
→ instances compose one another by identity/reference/relationship
→ higher-order behavior emerges from composition

Recursive identity does not require recursive duplication of implementation.

## 7. SCYON IS RECURSIVE

Recursion is a fundamental property of Scyon.

A Scyon-bearing identity may be composed from lower-tier identities that are themselves Scyon-bearing.

The recursion follows Savant's identity edifice rather than arbitrary object nesting.

The recursive chain may therefore express:

Trait Scyons
→ composed into Quirk Scyon

Quirk Scyons
→ composed into Prodigal Scyon

Prodigal Scyons
→ composed into Exile Scyon

and onward through higher qualifying tiers.

Every level remains independently graph-addressable.

Every constituent retains identity.

Every constituent retains lineage.

Every constituent retains provenance.

Every constituent remains reusable where authority permits.

No higher-tier identity absorbs or destroys the identities of its constituents.

## 8. SCYON IS EMERGENT

Scyon is also emergent.

Higher-tier capabilities do not have to be duplicated as new authoritative primitives when those capabilities deterministically emerge from lower-level composition.

A higher-tier Scyon is therefore not merely:

lower-tier Scyon
+ more methods

Instead, its effective behavior may emerge from:

- constituent Scyons;
- relationships among constituents;
- tier profile;
- shared abilities;
- Focal;
- Moods;
- Modus masks;
- authority;
- constraints;
- resources;
- current state;
- environment;
- deterministic projections.

Savant stores authoritative primitives and derives emergent behavior whenever deterministic derivation is sufficient.

---

## 9. INHERITANCE, RECURSION, AND EMERGENCE ARE DISTINCT

Scyon has three related but separate properties.

### 9.1 Inheritance

Higher-tier Scyons receive applicable shared Scyon functionality and tier-appropriate capabilities.

### 9.2 Recursion

Higher-tier Scyons compose identities that may themselves be Scyon instances.

### 9.3 Emergence

New higher-order capabilities may arise from composition without being separately duplicated as authoritative substance.

These concepts must not be conflated.

## 11. TRAIT SCYON

Trait is the lowest Scyon-bearing identity tier.

Trait Scyon represents the smallest independently living compositional identity requiring Scyon functionality.

It receives only the Scyon capabilities appropriate to Trait scope.

Trait Scyon must remain lightweight.

It must not carry Exile-scale machinery merely because the shared kernel can support it.

## 16. UNIVERSAL PRIORITY CAPABILITY

The priority system first developed through Niche is a universally useful Scyon ability at applicable tiers.

Its canonical modes are:

1. mandatory
2. salient
3. arbitrary

Mode semantics are invariant.

Mandatory:
minimum dependency-closed substance required for the requested outcome.

Salient:
mandatory plus materially important substance whose omission would significantly weaken the outcome.

Arbitrary:
the complete admissible expansion, including optional enhancements, alternatives, contingencies, optimizations, research, and other legitimate non-required substance.

The shared priority record contains:

1. intrinsic_priority
2. effective_priority
3. priority_context
4. priority_scope
5. promotion_chain
6. demotion_constraints
7. authority_source
8. classification_rationale
9. confidence
