## 1. purpose

this masterplan is intended to answer, in one place:

1.  what work is still ongoing, paused, blocked, unresolved, or
    proposed.
2.  what additional work is required to finish savant even when it was
    not previously expressed as a discrete task.
3.  what work is already completed with sufficient authority/evidence to
    call complete.
4.  what implementation exists but is not yet proven complete.
5.  what authority questions prevent safe implementation.
6.  what the next finite implementation boundary is.

the document is deliberately stricter than a source inventory.
filesystem presence is evidence only. a file, test, backup, projection,
receipt, or historical patch does not become authority merely because it
exists.

## 4. governing savant construction law

the governing construction remains:

`substantiate once → instance → compose → relate through typed segues → project`

required consequences:

-   authoritative primitives are stored once.
-   reusable instances reference substance rather than duplicate it.
-   typed segues expose relationships instead of hiding coupling.
-   derived information is projected deterministically rather than
    maintained as a second truth system.
-   lineage, provenance, dependencies, and dependents remain exposed
    where applicable.
-   history is preserved rather than destructively rewritten.
-   migration is reversible where representation changes can affect
    durable meaning.
-   external protocols normalize into savant-owned primitives at the
    boundary.
-   no subsystem absorbs another owner's semantic authority for
    convenience.
