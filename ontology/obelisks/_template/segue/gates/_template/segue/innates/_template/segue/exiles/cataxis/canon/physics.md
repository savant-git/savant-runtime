# cataxis physics

status: canonical physics architecture  
owner: cataxis  
primitive: spacetime

## purpose

cataxis is the sole owner of savant's physics layer.

other exiles may consume, parameterize, simulate, project, verify, or reason
over physical models, but physical substance remains cataxis-owned.

carbon consumes cataxis physics for simulation.

mobius consumes cataxis physics for physically constrained causal continuity.

zero supplies reusable mathematics without acquiring ownership of physics.

filament projects physical structures without becoming their authority.

## nine-law architecture

savant physics has exactly nine top-level laws.

the first six represent established physical principles at the abstraction
level implemented by the active cataxis model.

the final three are proprietary savant computational laws.

the latter are not claims about undiscovered laws of nature.

### law 1 — inertia

a body preserves its velocity unless a net external force changes its motion.

purpose:

- inertial propagation;
- detection of unsupported motion changes;
- stable baseline evolution.

### law 2 — dynamics

net force changes momentum.

for the constant-mass classical model:

f = ma

purpose:

- force integration;
- acceleration;
- momentum evolution;
- physical response.

### law 3 — interaction

mechanical pair interactions exchange equal and opposite momentum within the
modeled closed pair.

purpose:

- interaction symmetry;
- momentum exchange;
- detection of uncompensated pair forces.

### law 4 — conservation

applicable conserved quantities remain constant in an isolated modeled system.

cataxis explicitly tracks conservation rather than assuming numerical
integration preserved it.

initial implementation tracks:

- energy;
- linear momentum;
- modeled mass.

future attached models may extend conservation accounting without replacing
this law.

### law 5 — gravitation

mass-energy participates in gravitational interaction according to the active
physical model.

the baseline cataxis implementation provides classical inverse-square
newtonian gravitation.

strong-field relativistic behavior must not be fabricated by the classical
solver.

a future relativistic backend attaches beneath this law.

### law 6 — thermodynamics

physical evolution obeys energy accounting and thermodynamic constraints.

the baseline layer supports:

- internal energy;
- heat;
- work;
- absolute temperature;
- heat capacity;
- entropy transfer;
- nonnegative entropy generation.

thermodynamic sophistication may expand without changing ownership.

### law 7 — savant law of causal admissibility

no simulated physical transition may become admissible when its required
physical influence exceeds the causal limits of its active model.

this law exists to prevent a simulation engine from generating narratively
or computationally convenient but physically impossible transitions and then
allowing those transitions to contaminate downstream reasoning.

examples include:

- impossible travel;
- impossible signal propagation;
- unsupported instantaneous displacement;
- physically invalid ordering;
- propagation beyond the active model's causal envelope.

this law composes directly with mobius.

### law 8 — savant law of uncertainty inheritance

a derived physical state cannot silently possess greater certainty than the
uncertain primitives from which it was derived.

uncertainty is therefore part of physical lineage.

derived simulation state must preserve uncertainty or explicitly justify a
valid reduction through additional information.

this prevents false precision from appearing merely because a calculation was
performed.

### law 9 — savant law of resolution continuity

when an unresolved possibility resolves into one realized continuation,
physical continuity must remain valid across the resolution boundary.

mobius may collapse:

{a | b | c | d} -> c

but cataxis does not permit that operation to manufacture physically
unsupported state.

unrealized branches remain counterfactual state rather than being destroyed.

the law therefore binds mobius causal collapse to cataxis physical
continuity.

## model hierarchy

the nine laws are stable top-level contracts.

specific physical models are replaceable or composable implementations beneath
those contracts.

examples:

- classical mechanics;
- rigid-body mechanics;
- orbital mechanics;
- continuum approximations;
- thermodynamics;
- electromagnetism;
- relativistic models;
- quantum models.

the existence of a more sophisticated model must not require a new exile.

## fidelity

every simulation should be able to expose the fidelity of the physical model
being used.

a result generated by a simplified model must not masquerade as a result from
a higher-fidelity model.

physical fidelity may include:

- model identity;
- dimensionality;
- numerical tolerance;
- integration method;
- timestep;
- approximations;
- ignored effects;
- validity domain;
- uncertainty;
- conservation drift.

## physical impossibility

cataxis must be capable of returning:

physically inadmissible

rather than coercing every requested scenario into a successful simulation.

this is a core requirement.

## numerical integrity

physical simulation must distinguish physical effects from numerical artifacts.

future numerical solvers should expose:

- integrator;
- timestep;
- adaptive-step behavior;
- local error;
- global drift;
- conservation residuals;
- convergence;
- stability limits.

## uncertainty

physical quantities may carry uncertainty.

uncertainty must remain distinct from randomness.

randomness describes sampled variation.

uncertainty describes incomplete precision or knowledge.

carbon may sample uncertain states.

cataxis owns the physical interpretation of those states.

## determinism

where identical inputs, models, parameters, initial conditions, and random
seeds are supplied, cataxis should produce replayable results wherever the
underlying numerical method permits.

## provenance

physics projections should preserve:

- model;
- constants;
- initial state;
- boundary conditions;
- forces;
- constraints;
- timestep or interval;
- uncertainty;
- law results;
- numerical method;
- assumptions;
- ignored effects;
- resulting state;
- provenance;
- digest.

## dependency policy

the core nine-law layer uses the python standard library and existing cataxis
primitives.

specialized numerical libraries may attach as optional acceleration or
high-fidelity backends.

no external dependency becomes constitutional authority.

a library may improve computation.

it does not define savant physics.

## invariant

carbon simulates.

mobius preserves causal continuity.

zero supplies mathematics.

cataxis determines physical admissibility and physical evolution.

the nine laws are the stable contract connecting those responsibilities.
