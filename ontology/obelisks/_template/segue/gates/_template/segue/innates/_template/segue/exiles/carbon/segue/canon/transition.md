# carbon segue transition

## status

active

## owner

carbon

## lineage

carbon is savant's generalized evolution of the original oriel simulation concept.

oriel remains preserved as conceptual lineage.

carbon is the active generalized simulation owner.

## singular purpose

carbon owns advanced proprietary simulation.

the carbon segue projects carbon simulation capabilities across savant boundaries without transferring the authority of carbon, its sources, its consumers, or attached execution engines.

## ingress

carbon may receive references to:

- source instances
- admitted evidence
- accepted decisions
- declared parameters
- constraints
- events
- dependencies
- attached execution engines

references remain owned by their originating authority.

ingress does not make carbon authoritative over the referenced source.

## simulation

carbon may construct simulation definitions and instantiate graph-addressable simulations.

carbon owns:

- simulation definition
- simulation instantiation
- simulation execution
- simulation state
- simulation composition
- simulation branching
- counterfactual branching
- simulation checkpointing
- replay
- simulation comparison
- simulation interrogation
- parameter sweeps
- temporal progression
- event injection
- constraint projection
- causal trace projection
- uncertainty projection
- simulation receipts
- simulation provenance
- simulation lineage

## branches

simulation branches preserve ancestry.

a child branch does not mutate or replace its parent.

counterfactual branches remain explicitly distinguishable from observed history.

## execution engines

carbon may attach specialized execution engines.

an attached engine is an execution dependency, not carbon's identity.

engines remain replaceable.

delegating specialized execution does not transfer carbon simulation authority to the engine and does not transfer engine authority to carbon.

## composition

carbon simulations may be composed into other savant structures by reference.

carbon simulations may also compose other simulation instances.

higher-order simulations emerge from reusable instances rather than duplicated lower-level substance.

## avatar forge

avatar forge is a carbon-owned specialization for embodied identity simulation.

avatar forge uses carbon's generalized simulation substrate.

avatar forge does not define carbon's overall purpose.

## classification

simulation state is derived state.

simulation output is derived projection.

counterfactual output is derived projection.

a simulation receipt is evidence that a simulation operation occurred.

none of these classifications establish that a simulated result occurred in external reality.

## ownership invariant

the composed simulation system preserves these boundaries:

- source authority: source
- admitted evidence authority: admitting authority
- accepted decision authority: accepted authority
- simulation mechanics: carbon
- simulation state: carbon
- attached engine execution: attached engine
- consumer behavior: consumer
- external reality: external source

composition, projection, execution, branching, replay, comparison, interrogation, attachment, and delegation do not imply authority inheritance or authority transfer.
