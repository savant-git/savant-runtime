## identity

guise is savant's generalized character-intelligence specialization within carbon.

guise must remain capable of importing, substantiating, modeling, interrogating, comparing, simulating, and projecting arbitrary characters from arbitrary compatible corpora.

## purpose

guise converts character evidence, author-supplied primitives, imported writing, admitted source material, and explicitly requested generative hypotheses into provenance-preserving, temporally addressable, graph-addressable character models.

guise supports:

- character substantiation
- character extraction
- character creation
- character psychology
- physical characterization
- behavioral characterization
- character knowledge
- character belief
- character memory
- relationships
- developmental phases
- character state
- character arcs
- contradictions
- continuity
- counterfactual character simulation
- scene-conditioned behavior
- pressure-conditioned behavior
- character comparison
- author assistance
- deterministic character projections

## carbon relationship

carbon remains the generalized simulation owner.

guise is a carbon specialization.

guise does not redefine carbon.

guise does not transfer carbon authority.

guise uses carbon simulation, branching, counterfactual, replay, comparison, interrogation, uncertainty, lineage, provenance, and projection capabilities where applicable.

guise-generated hypotheses and simulations remain derived.

guise never silently converts simulation into canon.

guise never silently converts inference into canon.

guise never silently converts imported text into accepted authority.

## universal composition law

guise obeys savant's universal recursive composition architecture.

shard 00 is the single universal glyph substrate.

individual glyph substance is substantiated once at shard 00.

modules do not create private copies of universal glyphs.

higher structures emerge through reusable instances and composition.

conceptually:

glyph
→ glyph group
→ line
→ higher reusable compositions
→ module structures
→ semantic structures
→ character substantiations
→ projections

the exact physical storage representation of a edifice level does not create authority.

authority belongs to the governing authoritative instance graph.

## lexical and semantic separation

a semantic concept is not identical to the glyph sequence used to spell its label.

for example, the semantic category identified as insecurity is distinct from the lexical projection "insecurity".

this permits:

- aliases
- historical terminology
- translations
- renamed projections
- alternate display labels
- compatibility labels

without changing semantic identity.

## substantiation law

universal semantic substance is defined once.

character-specific substance is attached by reference.

for example:

psychology.category.insecurity

is reusable semantic structure.

a character's manifestation of insecurity is a character-specific substantiation referencing that reusable category.

character-specific evidence belongs to the character substantiation, not to the universal category.

## evidence law

evidence is substantiated independently from conclusions.

one evidence instance may support, challenge, contextualize, qualify, or contradict multiple character hypotheses.

evidence must not be duplicated merely because multiple categories consume it.

character conclusions must preserve their evidence references.

where applicable they must also preserve counterevidence.

- weak_inference

## immutable history

accepted historical substance is not silently rewritten.

corrections and changes are represented through supersession, temporal qualification, contradiction, or new authority.

generated projections may be replaced.

authoritative history may not be erased merely to simplify the current view.

## behavioral projection

guise behavioral projection must distinguish at minimum:

- highly_supported
- plausible
- productive_contradiction
- requires_substantiation
- continuity_conflict

silence is permitted when evidence is insufficient.

## falsification

guise must seek evidence that challenges character hypotheses.

a hypothesis supported only by confirming evidence must remain distinguishable from one that has survived meaningful counterevidence.

## alternative explanation

where evidence supports multiple explanations, guise preserves alternatives rather than selecting one without authority.

## character differential

when proposed behavior appears inconsistent, guise may project the minimum contextual, state, relationship, knowledge, or developmental change required to make the behavior coherent.

the differential is advisory and does not mutate canon.

## counterfactuals

counterfactual character testing occurs in carbon-derived branches.

counterfactual state never silently mutates accepted character substance.

branch ancestry must remain recoverable.

## negative evidence

guise distinguishes:

- not_observed
- observed_absent
- unknown

absence of evidence is not automatically evidence of absence.

## negative space

guise may model meaningful omissions, silences, avoidance, missing behavior, and unexpressed information only when evidence supports such interpretation.

silence must not be overinterpreted.

## behavioral entropy

guise may represent the breadth or narrowness of plausible behavior qualitatively.

it must not manufacture false numeric psychological precision.

## surprise without betrayal

guise may generate behavior that is surprising when the behavior remains retrospectively coherent with established character substance.

novelty alone is not sufficient.

## non-cliche restraint

guise must reject generic characterization where character-specific evidence does not distinguish the result from a broad stereotype.

generic adjectives are not authoritative characterization unless operationalized through evidence and behavior.

## compression test

a character model that can be replaced by a short list of generic adjectives without meaningful loss is insufficiently substantiated.

## adaptive dual use

traits may have adaptive and maladaptive manifestations.

guise must not classify a trait as universally beneficial or harmful when evidence supports context-dependent effects.

## pressure

guise models how character expression changes under pressure.

pressure may reveal, suppress, invert, intensify, or reorganize existing character tendencies.

pressure does not automatically reveal a supposedly "true" personality.

## regression

earlier behavioral strategies may recur under pressure without erasing later development.

## developmental phases

character identity may be projected across multiple temporal or developmental phases.

phases must emerge from evidence or explicit authorial definition.

guise must not invent phase boundaries merely for convenience.

## character memory

character memory is separate from canonical truth.

memory may be incomplete, false, distorted, forgotten, reinterpreted, or temporally inaccessible.

## author sovereignty

guise advises.

the author decides.

generative proposals remain proposals until accepted through governing authority.

guise may create possibilities from scratch only when generative operation is explicitly requested or authorized.

generated character substance must be labeled derived until accepted.

## extraction

extraction must preserve source identity and location where available.

extraction output is evidence or hypothesis, not automatically canon.

replaceable ai or analytical extraction providers may be attached.

## creation from scratch

guise may generate candidate character structures without source evidence when explicitly requested.
